# Morale, fatigue and battle weariness in other wargames

Research for the WARFRONT morale redesign (2026-10-23), web sources only. Editions
newer than expected: Warhammer 40,000 11th edition (core rules on Wahapedia, about June
2026) and Kings of War 4th edition (book December 2025). Chain of Command 2nd edition
(2025), Star Wars: Legion 2.0 (December 2025) and Necromunda: Skirmish (July 2026) exist,
but their morale text was not available, so those entries describe the previous edition.
"(unconfirmed)" marks what could not be checked.

## Where WARFRONT stands

- 2D6 against the unit's MORALE (lower is better); a failure makes it SUPPRESSED (-1 MOVE,
  -1 HIT, no Overwatch, OC 0) until it passes a Regroup test or is rallied. Morale never
  destroys a unit.
- Tests: the Command Phase Casualty Tally (Below Half-Strength) and Regroup; MORALE
  BREAKER, a COMMAND death, a Charge and specific rules. One test per activation. Rally
  Cry (2 CP) passes one.
- As of today, an attack that leaves a unit Below Half-Strength causes no test of its
  own.

## The games

### Warhammer 40,000

| Edition | Trigger | Roll | Result | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8th (2017) | Morale phase, any unit that lost models | D6 + models slain this turn vs Ld | one model flees per point over | none: models removed |
| 9th (2020) | same | D6 + models destroyed, pass at or under Ld (a natural 1 passes) | one flees, then Combat Attrition: each model flees on a 1 (-1 below half) | none |
| 10th (2023) | Command phase Battle-shock step: every unit Below Half-strength | 2D6 at or above best Ld | Battle-shocked until your next Command phase: OC 0, no Stratagems, no surge moves; a Fall Back costs models | expires; tests again if still below half |
| 11th (2026) | the same step tests units at or below half, and units already shocked | 2D6 vs Ld | shocked until a later test passes: OC "-", no stratagems, cannot start an action | the next Battle-shock test |

- 8th: criticised as an extra casualty step, and unit size mattered more than Leadership.
- 9th: Insane Bravery (2 CP, once per battle) auto-passes; seen as minor.
- 10th: a destroyed transport shocks its passengers; Insane Bravery is 1 CP once per
  battle. The intent was that morale is "no longer just a casualty multiplier": it is
  tied to objectives and CP.
- 11th: Insane Bravery cannot be used on a unit already shocked (it prevents shock, it
  does not cure it). The persistence makes deliberate battle-shock strategies viable.
  WARFRONT's Casualty Tally plus Regroup is now almost exactly this.

### Kill Team (2021, 2024)

No morale. An operative under half its wounds is Injured: 2024, -2" Move and Hit worse by
1; 2021, less Movement and BS/WS worse by 1.

### Age of Sigmar

- 3rd edition (2021): a Battleshock phase like 40k 8th (D6 + models slain vs Bravery, the
  excess flees). Inspiring Presence exempts a unit; Rally returns a model per 6.
- 4th edition (2024): battleshock and Bravery removed. A Control stat holds objectives,
  and the command-point economy replaces morale ("Battleshock has been removed, and there
  are meaningful Command Abilities in every round").

### Horus Heresy

3rd edition (2025) replaced its overlapping morale rules with **Tactical Statuses**:

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Pinned | cannot move |
| Suppressed | its shots fly wide |
| Stunned | hampered reactions |
| Routed | falls back toward its own edge, may leave the table |

All of them stop the unit capturing objectives, disorder its charges, and usually make it
fight last. Weapon traits (Pinning, Panic(X)), a lost combat and 25% losses in one phase
inflict them. A unit recovers on a Cool test (Leadership for Routed) at the end of its own
turn. Leadership is split into Ld, Willpower, Intelligence and Cool: "Some troops may be
easy to panic but hard to suppress."

### Warhammer: The Old World (2024)

- Break test after a lost combat: 2D6 + the combat-result difference vs Ld. Break and
  flee, fall back in good order, or give ground.
- Panic tests: 25% of the models lost in one phase outside combat; a friend of Unit
  Strength 5+ destroyed within 6"; a friend fleeing from combat nearby; a friend fleeing
  through. A failure falls back in good order above 50% strength, else flees.
- Fleeing units move 2D6" and die if they leave the table or are caught. Rally is a
  Leadership test in the Strategy phase: -1 below 50% strength, and only a double 1 below
  25%.

### Bolt Action (3rd edition, 2023)

- Every enemy hit adds a **pin**, casualties or not: -1 to hit and -1 morale per pin.
- Order test: 2D6 at or under morale (8, 9 or 10 by quality). Double 6 is FUBAR; a
  failure puts the unit Down. Pins at or above morale destroy the unit.
- A Rally order tests ignoring pins and removes them all. Units Down at the turn's end lose
  D3 pins (edition unconfirmed).

### Chain of Command (1st edition)

- **Shock** per hit (or a kill, by cover); running adds Shock. -1 firing die per 2 Shock,
  and Shock comes off movement dice.
- Pinned when Shock exceeds the men; Broken at double, falling back 6" + 2D6" and routing
  unless rallied. Leaders remove Shock with command initiative.
- **Force Morale**: an army track (8 to 11) that drops on setbacks (a team lost or broken,
  a leader hit). At 4 the side has one command die fewer, at 3 two, at 2 two and loses a
  jump-off point, at 1 three, and at 0 it routs.

### Flames of War (4th edition)

Pinned Down at 5 hits in one shooting step (8 for big units; tanks exempt): no advance, and
it shoots at its moving rate. Each turn: remount bailed-out tanks, rally pinned units on
Rally/Motivation (Fearless 3+, Confident 4+, Reluctant 5+), Unit Last Stand (down to 1 tank
or gun, or 2 infantry teams), Formation Last Stand. Morale is split into separate ratings.

### Kings of War

- 3rd edition: damage stays on the unit all game. After a phase in which it took damage,
  it tests 2D6 + total damage against Nerve (Waver/Rout, e.g. 11/13): Wavered can only
  halt or back off, Routed is removed. Inspiring rerolls a rout; Fearless never wavers.
- 4th edition (December 2025): one Nerve value. Shooting can no longer rout a unit unless
  it is already Devastated: a failed shooting test Wavers it. Mantic's aim is to avoid
  "a key unit deleted in a single volley".

### One Page Rules: Grimdark Future (version 3)

- A Quality test at the end of any activation that left the unit at half or below, or
  after a lost melee. **Shaken**: idle, strikes back as Fatigued, always fails morale,
  cannot contest objectives. One whole idle activation clears it.
- Failing a melee test at half strength or below routs the unit.
- **Fatigue**: after charging or striking back it hits only on unmodified 6s in melee for
  the round.
- A more forgiving Shaken (April 2024) was reverted a month later for added complexity.

### Star Wars: Legion

Each ranged attack adds a **suppression** token (from April 2025 not if every wound was
prevented). At courage it loses an action; above twice courage it panics: no actions, drops
objectives, cannot score (threshold unconfirmed). It rallies at the start of its
activation, and every unit loses a token in the End Phase. In 2023 panicked units stopped
fleeing the board and instead could not score, since wiping a unit had been the only way to
deny it.

### Infinity (N5)

- Guts Roll after an attack that forced a save: on a failed Willpower roll the trooper
  moves 2" out of the line of fire, into cover, or goes Prone.
- Loss of Lieutenant: every trooper becomes Irregular until a new one is named.
- Retreat!: at 25% of the list or less, skills are restricted and troopers may leave.

### SAGA (2nd edition)

No tests, only **fatigue**: +1 for each move, charge or shot after a unit's first that
turn, after each melee, and when a friend dies nearby. At 3 it is exhausted and can only
Rest. The **opponent spends your fatigue**: to cut your move, cancel an activation, or
shift armour in melee and shooting. Rest removes one.

### Black Powder (2nd edition)

Shaken at casualties equal to Stamina (usually 3). A break test (2D6 with modifiers) after
excess casualties, a lost melee, artillery hits and so on: 4 or less, destroyed; 5 to 6,
retires disordered; 7 or more, holds. A brigade breaks when half its units are broken or
shaken (unconfirmed).

### Mordheim and Necromunda

- Mordheim: a Rout test at 25% of the warband out of action; a failure loses the game.
- Necromunda (2018): any ranged hit Pins. A friend seriously injured or taken out of action
  forces a Nerve test within 3"; Broken fighters run for cover and Rally at the end phase
  (+1 per friend nearby). The gang's Bottle test is D6 + its casualties against gang size.

### Epic Armageddon

Formations collect **blast markers**: +1 each time they are shot at (even with no
casualties), +1 per unit lost, +1 per failed action test. Each marker silences one unit's
fire. At markers equal to its units the formation breaks and withdraws, and further
markers become hits. A rally test at the end phase removes half the markers; the Marshal
action removes more.

### Video games

- **Total War**: a morale meter moved by casualties, flanks, the general, fatigue and
  routing friends; a unit wavers, then routs; the general rallies. Fatigue tiers (Tired to
  Exhausted) are stat multipliers. Critique: units fight on to about 50% losses, against
  roughly 10% historically.
- **Company of Heroes 2**: sustained fire Suppresses (crawl, slower fire), then Pins (no
  move or fire, reduced damage taken, may still retreat).
- **Battle Brothers**: Confident, Steady, Wavering, Breaking, Fleeing, checked on
  events (exact numbers unconfirmed).
- **XCOM 2: War of the Chosen**: Will drains in a mission. Tired, then Shaken soldiers panic
  more and aim worse; Will recovers between missions.

### Strategic war weariness (battle-length analogues only)

- Hearts of Iron IV: Organisation is a second track beside Strength. It drains in combat,
  the division withdraws at 0, and it regenerates out of combat.
- Europa Universalis IV: morale drains daily in combat; an army retreats at 0 and recovers
  slowly.
- Civilization VI: purely strategic (amenities); no battle analogue.

## Patterns

**Triggers**
1. A threshold checked at a fixed time (40k 10th/11th, WARFRONT's Casualty Tally).
2. A threshold crossed mid-turn (OPR, Old World, Horus Heresy, Bolt Action): immediate,
   but more rolls mid-sequence. This is the test WARFRONT just removed.
3. Per attack, with no roll: a marker (Bolt Action pins, Legion suppression, Epic blast
   markers, Chain of Command shock, Necromunda pinning). Every attack matters without a
   test per attack.
4. Events: a leader lost, a friend lost nearby, a lost melee, a destroyed transport,
   weapon traits (MORALE BREAKER).
5. Self-inflicted exertion: SAGA, Chain of Command running, OPR charging, Total War
   running.

**Outcomes**
- Stat penalties, often per marker (Bolt Action, Chain of Command, Kill Team, SUPPRESSED).
- Action denial (Legion, Kings of War, OPR, Epic, Horus Heresy Pinned).
- Objective denial (40k 10th/11th, Horus Heresy, OPR, Legion; Age of Sigmar 4th replaced
  morale with Control). The current consensus at GW, Atomic Mass Games and OPR.
- Forced movement (Old World, Black Powder, Chain of Command, Epic, Necromunda, Infinity).
- Removal (40k 8th/9th, Age of Sigmar 3rd, Kings of War, Bolt Action, Black Powder, OPR):
  the most criticised, and being walked back (Kings of War 4th, Legion 2023).
- Army-level effects (Chain of Command command dice, Infinity orders, Flames of War
  formations, Black Powder brigades, Necromunda bottling).

**Recovery**: a test at a set point (40k 11th, Old World, Epic, Necromunda, Horus Heresy);
spending the activation (Bolt Action Rally, OPR idle, SAGA Rest); leaders and friends
nearby (Chain of Command, Total War, Necromunda); passive decay (Legion, Epic, Bolt Action
Down); expiry (40k 10th); harder at low strength (Old World, Epic).

**Weariness over a battle**: markers that persist until an action clears them; exertion
fatigue with a cap and a rest (SAGA, Total War); army-level erosion (Chain of Command Force
Morale, Infinity Retreat!, Total War army losses, XCOM Will).

## What fits WARFRONT (morale never destroys; a failure means SUPPRESSED)

1. **It already matches 40k 11th.** Two differences to decide: 11th tests "at or below"
   half strength (WARFRONT: fewer than half), and 11th's auto-pass is once per battle and
   cannot clear a unit already shocked (Rally Cry is once per Battle Round).
2. **OC 0 is the strongest non-lethal lever.** Horus Heresy shows several statuses sharing
   it; SUPPRESSED could become one of a family.
3. **Pressure before half strength without per-attack tests**: a marker per attack (pins,
   suppression), each worsening the next Morale Test, decaying one a round, perhaps a
   second tier at double the threshold. It fits one test per activation.
4. **FATIGUED from exertion** (SAGA, OPR): gained by charging, fighting, running, capped,
   removed by a Rest, no roll. Letting the opponent spend it (SAGA) is flavourful but adds
   a sub-economy.
5. **Recovery choices**: an idle activation that clears SUPPRESSED instead of the Regroup
   roll (OPR, Bolt Action); bonuses from leaders or friends nearby (Chain of Command,
   Necromunda); Regroup harder at low strength (Old World, Epic).
6. **Battle weariness through CP**: Chain of Command's Force Morale, command dice shrinking
   as the army bleeds, maps onto CP. A COMMAND death or a broken squad would be the events.
7. **Patterns that conflict**: removal (contradicts "morale never destroys"), random
   behaviour (XCOM), forced movement (beyond "a failure means SUPPRESSED").
8. **Cautions**: OPR's nuanced Shaken was reverted for complexity in a month; Kings of War
   4th took routing off shooting; Legion turned fleeing into scoring denial; Age of Sigmar
   4th dropped morale. The trend is fewer removals, more denial of actions and scoring.
