Battle report: a Fall Back's outcome counts the damage its move drew

The Fall Back record read what followed a decision from the round's Action Phase
only (fallback.cjs outcome), so the Overwatch a Fall Back move drew was missed:
the requisitioned Cultist Troopers' Fall Back said "after: lost 0 HP" though the
Fulminator Squad's Overwatch did 4 damage during that move (Champion League D1-M08
R2). It now counts the rest of the Movement Phase from the decision on - the page
notes the decision's place in the record (seq) - with the FALL BACK test's own
damage counted once, as the test's. A record made before the seq counts the whole
Movement Phase.
