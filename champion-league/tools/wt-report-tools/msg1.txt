Battle report: credit fields, side events as they happened, keep costs apart

Four bugs the Champion League Day 1 replay found in the credit ledger and the
numbers built on it.

- A field's damage went to no unit. A BURNING FIELD deals its Cleanup damage with
  no attacker and books the damage, its BURNED and a death one call after another
  (phases/action_cleanup.js), outside any call the record's actors cover: "by null
  src null" in D1-M01 (Sunburst) and D1-M04 (Phlegraean Fields killed the Eire
  Pathfinders). page_credit.js now notes who placed each field (placeFieldObject,
  inside the use or attack that leaves it) and credits the field's damage, its
  BURNED stacks and its kills to that unit, as a combat point named BURNING FIELD
  by its rule.
- The armies were read once Round 1 was under way (record.cjs META), so a Round 1
  Troop Requisition counted as deployed, not as a reinforcement (D1-M21, D1-M24).
  The page now reads them at deployment (R.deployed); summarize.cjs trims an older
  record's meta.armies to its deployment snapshot.
- "Enemy units destroyed" credited a side with the units its enemy lost to its own
  fire (D1-M20: 7 : 6, two of them the Popinjay Guild's own stray shots). Each
  army's losses are now split by who destroyed them (sides.cjs deaths), and the
  headline tiles, the RP-trade charts and the attrition tips count only the
  enemy's kills.
- Every credit was sided by its unit's final owner, so a seized unit's damage
  before its seizure read as friendly fire (D1-M14, Temple Levy 1). Each credit now
  carries both sides as they stood; an older record's are read from the combat
  point each happened in. A unit that fought for both sides has a row for each.
- A cost a unit's own rule makes it pay is no friendly fire (D1-M18: Desperate
  Worship's 2 HP read "ranged FRIENDLY FIRE ... -> Armored Gashadokuro"). A cost
  paid inside a combat point is kept as its `costs`, apart from its losses; a
  credit a unit took from itself is a cost; a loss on the attacker itself is no
  friendly fire in an older record either. The digest lists costs paid apart.
