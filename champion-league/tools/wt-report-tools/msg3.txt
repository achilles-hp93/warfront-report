Battle report: a table grant, and what a use queued, are what it bought

Two uses priced "nothing - no change" though they worked (Champion League Day 1).

- The Tri-Banner Chaplain's Three Standards (D1-M06) put its pick in
  G.tableGrants, which the table_active predicate reads: no modifier, status or HP,
  so every round's use had `changes: {}`, though the passive record showed the
  Ruthenian Standard's +1 MOVE on five units. A table grant is now a lever kind
  (page_effects_levers.js): the use's changes name the standard it granted, and the
  attacks and moves of the units it reaches are priced with it and with the grant
  left out for one read (page_effects_moves.js asks a lever kind's moves()).
- Blessing of Sacred Rabies (D1-M07 R4) queues a compelled attack that is settled
  after the cast (combat/compelled_attacks.js); the cast was diffed only as its own
  call ended, so the Bounded Ifrit's strike on its own Saladin and the SUPPRESSED it
  ended on were no one's. page_effects_deferred.js gives a use the work it queued -
  a compelled attack, an effect scheduled for a later checkpoint (Black Vortex of
  Dis) - and diffs the board into the use as that work settles (diffInto, factored
  out of endUse), with each compelled strike a gain of its own.
