Battle report: a unit holding an objective is not idle

The user's ruling (2026-10-29): "if they are on an objective then they are not
idle". The activity record counted a round idle whenever a unit spent no APt
(unitHeldFireInRound), so every unit that stood on an objective and held it read as
idle - the inaction score and the idle lists said the opposite of what it did.

page_activity.js now asks, just before the round's objectives score, which units
stand in an Objective Area with their OC counting there (getEffectiveOC above 0; an
OC anchored on the objective, its anchor's). Each held ground that round: an action
of its own kind, `objective`, and not an idle round. activity.cjs counts it among the
action scores; the page, the digest and the batch charts show it ("Held an
objective"), and the skill's definition of the inaction score says so.

Each activity entry now carries its unit's side as it happened, and a unit a
seizure took over has a row for each side: Temple Levy 1's Round 1 idle round, while
it was still Uruk's, was listed as the Veiled Court's (D1-M14). An older record's
entries are sided by the unit's own combat point or move in that phase, else by the
snapshot at the end of the round.
