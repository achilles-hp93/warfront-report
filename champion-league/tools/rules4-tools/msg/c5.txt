A granted Charge is a Charge: it locks ranged weapons and counts for missions

The user's ruling (2026-10-30): "granted charge are charges so they should have the
same rule". A granted as_charge move (core/movement/reposition.js; Insatiable Rage)
called markCharge but set no charge_ranged_lock, so the unit, and a pair's CHARACTER,
could still shoot. lockRangedAfterCharge (core/movement/charge.js) is now the one place
a performed Charge is marked, asked by the Charge roll and by the granted Charge; on
arrival beside an enemy the granted Charge goes through resolveChargeOnArrival, so the
missions that count a Charge count it too (it already had the charged mark and the +3
INI strike). Like every granted move it still provokes no Reaction.

Test: ops/grant_move's as_charge section, on a joined pair.
