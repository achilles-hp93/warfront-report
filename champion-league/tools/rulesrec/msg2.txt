Battle report: a round a unit acted in, or had no APt left to spend, is not idle

The activity record counted a round idle when the engine's held-fire test
passed (unitHeldFireInRound: there with APt from the round's start, none
spent), which only means the unit spent no APt. So a round in which it fired a
FREE weapon (D1-M01 R2 Spahbed's Firebreath; D1-M15 R2 and R4 Michaelite
Brethren 2's Paradiso Pistol) was both a ranged action and idle, and so was a
0 APt ability's; and a round in which an enemy rite set its APt to 0 before it
acted (D1-M01 R3, Daemonium Meridianum on Aswaran 1 and Spahbed) read as idle
although it had no APt to spend.

page_activity.js now counts a round idle only when the unit also took no
action that round (any activity entry but an Overwatch shot, which keeps its
own credit; holding an objective is one, as before) and still had APt when its
side passed the rest of the Action Phase (at the round's end, if its side never
passed). The engine's held-fire test and Overwatch readiness are unchanged, and
an ADVANCE-only round is still idle: both are open questions for the user.

Across the check run's 24 battles this drops 12 of 651 idle entries: 10 rounds
with a recorded action that spent no APt (5 ranged attacks, 5 abilities) and
the 2 of M01 R3. Replayed on the check run's own build with this recorder,
M01 and M15 match their records move for move and drop exactly these (5 and
3); the idle entries with no `why` in M01 R2/R3 and M15 R2/R4 were among them.

Test: a synthetic page in battle_report_tests/activity.cjs - a FREE attacker,
a unit zeroed before its side passed, one zeroed after, an Overwatch shooter,
and a side that never passed.
