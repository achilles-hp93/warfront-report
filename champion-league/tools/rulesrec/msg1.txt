Rules: a joined pair's CHARGE locks its CHARACTER's ranged weapons too

The core rules (Joined units) say Advance, Fall Back and Charge count for both
units of a pair, and a Charge, made or failed, prevents the unit from using
ranged weapons for the rest of the Battle Round. The Charge roll set its
charge_ranged_lock round mark on the charging squad alone
(core/movement/charge.js resolveChargeRoll), so the joined CHARACTER kept its
ranged weapons: in Champion League D1-M20 Round 4 Caesar fired Fulmen Iovis in
the Action Phase after his Ironthunder Vanguard charged. The check run's 24
battles hold ten such shots, by joined CHARACTERs after their pair's charge in
eight battles (nine in the Action Phase, one an Overwatch shot).

The roll now marks every unit of the footprint (footprintMembers), so every
reader of the mark answers for both: the ranged lock in weaponUseBlockReason
(combat/attack_checks.js), the ordered-Charge gate in canDeclareCharge, and
fire_after_enemy_charge, which still asks only the charger. ADVANCE
(advanceUnit) and FALL BACK (fallBack's fellBackThisRound, markFellBackFrom)
already marked both units.

Test: the successful-CHARGE test of the movement core suite now checks the
attached Centurion's Helios Pistol is refused after its squad's Charge.
