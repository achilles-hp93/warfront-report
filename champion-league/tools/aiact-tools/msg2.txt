AI: an ADVANCE is priced on every action it takes from its units; a pair's CHARGE gives up both units' shots

The user's ruling (2026-10-30, round 4): "a unit that only advance is a
problem too. It lost out on shooting usually. We need to price whether an
advance is justified." The first-turn push checked only the moving unit
(its move-fire penalty, the Artillery role, its own shot) and the ordinary
ADVANCE only the mover's own damage from where it stood; a joined
CHARACTER's shots and casts never counted. 84 of Day 1's 91 pairs with a
CHARACTER advanced in Round 1, and 45 of those CHARACTERs made no shot or
cast that round.

One price, ai/movement/forgone_actions.js (aiForgoneActions), for both moves:
- an ADVANCE: for every unit of the footprint, what its action this round
  is worth had the footprint walked to the walk's square (or stood), less
  what it is worth after the ADVANCE on the square each roll of its Movement
  Dice takes it to, at that roll's chance. Each is the AI's own reading:
  the best shot this round (the old aiChargeForgoneShot's estimate, now at
  enemies in the army's sight this round - _aiEnemiesInSightThisRound, the
  before-moving shot rule's - and with the weapons the rules let fire), or
  the Action Phase's best candidate there (aiUnitBestAction, read as the
  unit's own activation: a cast, an ability). The rules decide what is left
  after the ADVANCE, read through withFootprintAdvanced
  (movement/default_actions.js, marking the pair as advanceUnit does): an
  ASSAULT weapon, everything for a unit a rule lets act (Legacy of Rakhsh);
- a CHARGE: every footprint unit's best shot - a pair's CHARGE locks both
  units' ranged weapons (core/movement/charge.js since 9f66b27a), and the
  charge score and the COUNTERCHARGE's worth took off the charging unit's
  alone.

aiAdvanceWorth (ai/movement/advance_plan.js) is the ADVANCE's ground over its
dice (aiAdvanceExpectedScore, the destination score: an Objective Area, the
targets, charges and objective of the next round) less those actions at
W_CHARGE_COMBAT, the weight a CHARGE's lost shot has against the ground. The
push and the ordinary ADVANCE go only when it beats the walk's own square
(_aiChargeFloorGround, as a charge must); the push carries it as `worth`,
which a CHARGE must beat to be made instead, and an HQ command's price of the
move reads it (aiUnitMoveChoiceGain). The Artillery-only rule and the 1.0
margin are gone into it; the ordinary ADVANCE is weighed only when no walk is
taken. aiAdvanceRollSquares is the one roll -> square reading the ground and
the actions are priced over.

Note: there is no rule that a unit which Advanced has OC 0 (core rules,
Objectives: only an air-deployed unit contributes 0 OC that round), so the
ADVANCE square's objective term counts this round's control, as the engine
scores it.

Tests: 132 - the push goes only when its ground beats the shots it gives up
(the gun, and a squad that is no artillery, priced alike; it used to say a
squad that is no artillery pushes whatever it gives up), and a pair's push is
priced on its CHARACTER's shot from the walk's square (the Spahbed's
Firebreath; the Aswaran alone, who act after an ADVANCE, push for the same
ground). 160 - a pair's CHARGE gives up the Spahbed's shot beside the
Aswaran's, and the charge score takes off both. With the price's gate patched
out of the built page the two 132 cases fail ("the gun walks and shoots",
"the pair walks"); with aiForgoneActions summing the moving unit alone the
pair cases fail in 132 and 160. 158, 233 and the push-against-charge case
stand the price in (aiForgoneActions 0, the push's worth), as their own
questions are the scout rule and the Double Time's dice; 187 reads the renamed
helper.
