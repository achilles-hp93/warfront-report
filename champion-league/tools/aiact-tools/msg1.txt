AI: whether a unit may still act asks the ability rules, so a QUICK ability at 0 APt is offered

aiUnitCanStillAct (ai/tactical/focus_fire.js) asked the attack rules for an
attack since 9f66b27a, but its other branch was still a hand check -
APt > 0, not attacked, no ability or action used - so a QUICK ability the
rules allow at 0 APt was never offered unless an attack was possible too.

The branch now asks the rules' own checks: an Action Phase ability by
canUseAbility (the check useAbility, the ability dispatcher, makes), a cast
by riteCasterBlockReason (the casting button's), Channel Mana by
canChannelMana. They are read as the unit's own Action Phase activation
would read them (aiAsOwnActivation, ai/action/choose.js: the Action Phase,
its player active, its activation open), since the hold of an activation in
progress lasts only until it ends; aiUnitBestAction reads through the same
frame. A QUICK ability's limit and charges are the same check's to count, so
a unit that has spent it has nothing left and is not offered it again.

The focus-fire pool counts a friend only when it may still attack
(attackerMeansBlockReason): a unit whose action to come is a QUICK ability
adds no fire. aiIsActionPhaseAbility (ai/action/collect.js) is the one
definition of an ability the Action Phase acts with, read by the candidate
collection and by the check above.

Test: 371 gains the case - the Spahbed at 0 APt, its FREE Firebreath spent,
may still use its QUICK Boar of the Empire (a legal candidate), also while
another unit's activation is open, and not once its charge is spent. With
the old hand check patched back into the built page (both engine copies) the
test fails: "a unit at 0 APt with a QUICK ability the rules allow may still
act".
