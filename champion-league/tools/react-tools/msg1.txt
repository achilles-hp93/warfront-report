AI: every Charge, ranged attack and Overwatch prices the Reactions it draws

The user's decision (round 4, "b"): build one Reaction estimate from the AI's
existing defender decisions and use it for every charge - ordinary, ordered and
granted - and route Insatiable Rage and Sabre Signal through the normal charge
scoring. And the user's standard, "no obviously stupid mistake ... applies to
everything": the same estimate serves ranged attacks and Overwatch.

No action priced the Reactions it draws; W_REACTION_AVOID was read by nothing but
the debug slider. Alea Iacta lost three units to the Silver Arrow Brethren's Return
Volley (Champion League D1-M20): Legionnaire Squad 2 shot Akritic Outriders for 2 HP
(R1), Champion of Mars at 3 HP and Legionnaire Squad 1 at 4 HP Overwatched for
nothing (R2, R3), and the Volley destroyed each.

Each Reaction is asked as the reacting side's own AI decides it, on the same
board - never a copy of the decision:
- the Countercharge (ai/movement/charge_reactions.js): the offer the CHARGE would
  make (counterchargeOfferContext, its candidates by the rules'
  counterchargeCandidates, now shared with offerCountercharge) put to the seam's
  own AI choice (aiInterjectionAnswer: the command's CP, enabler and once-a-round
  gates, the 1 CP kept, aiCounterchargeRank). It lands at the odds of the square it
  declares (counterchargePlan) and cancels the Charge, and the charger fights the
  countercharger instead (aiCounterchargeOwn's fight). A Countercharge's own Charge
  draws none.
- each OVERWATCH at a charger's landing square: the watcher's weighing
  (aiOverwatchAttackOptions, split out of _aiChooseOverwatchAttack) at the chance
  its softmax draw takes each attack (aiSoftmaxProbabilities, split out of
  aiSoftmaxSelect);
- each Fire After Enemy Charge (aiFireAfterChargeAttack, the interjection's pick),
  after a failed Charge as well;
- each attack a ranged attack's unit_attacked event offers back at the attacker
  (Return Volley): eventEffectsAnswering (abilities/runtime/events.js) asks the
  dispatcher's own gates - split out as _eventEffectAnswers, with the once-per-round,
  -battle and -activation allowances peeked, not spent - and the offered unit takes
  the attack its interjection's AI takes (aiEventRangedAttackPick, with
  eventRangedAttackOffered, the op's own offer check).

ai/tactical/drawn_shots.js is the one price: the shots' HP (the rules' expectation)
at the unit's worth, and more for the chance they finish it (aiKillBonusFactor, the
kill bonus _aiScoreAttack applies, now one function). _aiPickBestCompositionAttack
takes it off every ranged attack, aiOverwatchAttackOptions off every Overwatch, and a
Charge off its score through _aiChargeReactedScore with the Countercharge's chance and
the share of its fight its shots kill - at W_DRAWN_REACTIONS (the renamed
W_REACTION_AVOID, 1.0: their expected cost). A Countercharge's own worth prices the
Reactions its Charge draws the same way.

Kept cheap: a Charge asks them only of squares that pass its bound and floor, and a
square they leave unable to win skips the Fall Back question (_aiChargeReactedBound,
the Countercharge included); the squares are now asked best bound first, so the best
is found early and bounds the rest - the same pick, ties to the first square; every
answer is kept for the decision; a side that could not react at all costs one scan
of its units per decision. The build's event-gate scan (generator/vocabulary.py)
reads the shared gate function as well as the dispatcher.

aiOrderedChargeSquare (ai/movement/ordered_charge.js) weighs a granted or ordered
CHARGE on the charge score: Insatiable Rage's square (no roll, compulsory: the best
square, whatever it is worth) and Sabre Signal's run and its worth when the ability
is weighed (with its roll, made only when it beats the unit's ground). They took the
plain ground score of a granted move.

Tests: 187 (a Charge into an Overwatch-Ready gun line is priced lower than with the
gun line spent, by the watcher's own expected Overwatch), 173 (a granted Charge
leaves no unit free to countercharge when another square is nearly as good), 167
(Sabre Signal is worth its charge score and its run goes there), grant_move (Sabre
Signal's worth counts the Countercharge), 050 (a Countercharge's own Charge prices
its Overwatch and draws no Countercharge), 165 (a ranged attack is worth the Return
Volley it draws less), 255 (an Overwatch that draws a Volley worth more is held),
ability_runtime 240 (what an attack would draw is asked spending nothing).
