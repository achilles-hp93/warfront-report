# Champion League Day 1 - AI and identity audit, D1-M13 to D1-M18

Sources: `digest.cjs <name> --log`, `effects.cjs --uses`, `fallback.cjs`, `passivesRecord` (run on report.json),
report.json snapshots/moves/resources, unit/faction/magic data under `data/editions/fantasy/`. Quotes are copied
from the digest or log. "[in notes]" marks an item the Day notes page already lists; everything else is new.
Objective Areas on CROSSROADS: centre (7,7) = cols 6-8 rows 6-8; left (1,7) = cols 0-2 rows 6-8; right (13,7) =
cols 12-14 rows 6-8. Enemy half for A = rows 0-6, for B = rows 8-14 (`rowInEnemyHalf`).

## Cross-battle summary (patterns)

1. **Charging a target that has not moved yet buys nothing.** A charge in the Movement Phase lets the target Fall
   Back in its own later activation, and by rule the charger then cannot use ranged weapons that round. M13: the
   two Palatine Custos (140 RP) charged five times and never made one attack (they also lost their FREE pistols
   each time) [in notes: "five charges, never attacked"]. The same thing happened to Terek Shashka Riders 1 and 2
   (M15), Skeletal Spearman 1 (M16, twice), Veche Levy 1 (M17) and Babylonian Assassin 1 (M14).
2. **Units walk off objectives they hold, or leave them empty.** M13 League Hoplite 1 (5 VP swing) [in notes],
   Argyraspides 2. M14 two Cultist units off the left (5 VP: the objective, plus TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY for B).
   M14 Lamassu Bastion [in notes], which never went back while B passed whole Movement Phases (6 VP; B lost by 5).
   M16 Skeletal Archer 1 off the centre it held alone (3 VP). M17 Sidhe Wardens 3 twice off an uncontested right
   objective (4 VP) and Veche Levy 1's "costly" Fall Back off the centre [in notes]. M15 Ivan Sirko's pair off the
   centre to charge 10-RP Levies.
3. **Countercharge throws artillery into elite melee** and hands over ASSASSINATION [in notes]: the Firelake Warden
   into Genghis (M16) and the Tsuchigumo into the Berserkers (M18). M18 was lost by 3, and the Tsuchigumo gave
   up 4 VP, so this one decided the battle.
4. **Fall Back tests at 6+ are routine.** Argyraspides 1 took three of them (M13: 9 True Damage, 3 models). Three
   units died to their own test: Veche Levy 2 and the Fomorian (M17), and the War Onmyoji (M18), the last one
   straight after B paid 2 CP to Rally it. Ramesses and Skeletal Spearman 2 also took 6+ tests (M16). Fall Back
   destinations often stayed inside the chargers' reach (M13 Argyraspides 1, M14 Temple Levy 3), so the unit was
   charged and tested again.
5. **Magic is mostly unspent.**
   - Casters gathered Mana and never cast: the Asipu Priest (M14) [in notes]; Ivan Sirko (M15, 4 Mana);
     Ramesses (M16, 3-7 Mana); the Black Shaman (M16, 20 Mana) [in notes]; the War Onmyoji (M18, never even
     channelled). The Queen Consort channelled in the final round (M14).
   - Two armies built a resource they could never spend: the Protectores' Pietas reached Tier IV with no MAGIC
     CASTER (M13), and the Chonae Wing banked 29 Mana with no caster (M15) [in notes].
   - **Tithe of Torment looks unimplemented** (Infernal Mana "1 per HP lost"): its core_system.json has no
     `income` or `effects`, and no engine code reads it. In M14 the Veiled Court took dozens of HP of damage and
     got Mana only from Channel Mana.
6. **Commands and abilities bought nothing, and missions were missed.** Double Time, Scouting Order and Secure
   Objective often bought nothing [in notes]. COMMAND AUTHORITY expired unclaimed twice (M14, M18) while units
   stood in the enemy half and the HQ Commands went to units in the army's own half. In M18 a Double Time on a
   joined CHARACTER alone gave the pair nothing, because the pair moves at the lower MOVE. Other gaps: abilities
   the AI scored and still left idle (Thaumata, Champion of The Pit, Grand Prince of The Rus, Whispers of Forbidden
   Pleasure, Scattered Soulfire, Blinding Flash), the Covenant Miracles (M15), and Mandata Principum, which chained
   to a unit with nothing to do three times (M13).
7. **The AI did not take VP in rounds that decided the battle.** M14 R5-R6: B passed its whole Movement Phase
   while the right objective stood empty and BREAK CONTACT was in hand. M18 R6: B passed its Movement Phase,
   lost the left objective, and never tried TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET.
8. **Expensive units stood idle.** Palatine Custos x2 (M13). The Veiled Queen (70 RP) never acted in 6 rounds
   (M14). The Masque of Carnality (55 RP) did 1 damage. Gilgamesh dealt 0. Heron of Alexandria was idle R3-R4.
   Argyraspides 2 + Asklepian Medic and League Hoplite 2 never acted (M13). The Sphinx Construct did 0 damage
   (M16).
9. **Primary handling.**
   - M15 RAISE THE STANDARD was planted in A's own corner at (0,10). No unit ever went there: 0/10 VP, and the
     standard was torn down in R3.
   - M13: for FORLORN HOPE, B twice named the safest A unit.
10. **Identity bugs and gaps.**
   - Temptation of Earthly Delight leaves the defector joined to the enemy CHARACTER, so A's own Assassin killed
     the defected unit (M14) [in notes].
   - Insatiable Rage's compulsory charge pulled the Avatar of Wrath off the objective it held (M18, 2 VP).
   - Albion's Flow of Magic gave nothing on CROSSROADS in four rolls (M17).
   - The Delphic Omen 4 was spent in a battle with no Panhellenic melee (M13).
   - Black Earth Underfoot never fired, because the AI declined every Action-Phase Riders move (M15).
