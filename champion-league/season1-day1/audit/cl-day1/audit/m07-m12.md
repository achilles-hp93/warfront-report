# Champion League Day 1, D1-M07 to D1-M12: AI and identity audit

Sources: `digest.cjs` (with and without `--log`), `effects.cjs --uses`, `fallback.cjs`, the
report.json snapshots, and the unit, ability, faction and magic data. CROSSROADS is a flat 15x15
board (its elevation grid is all 0). Objectives are at (1,7) 2 VP, (7,7) 3 VP and (13,7) 2 VP. An
Objective Area is Chebyshev distance 1 or less. A deploys on rows 12-14 and B on rows 0-2; row 7
belongs to neither half. The battle ends when a side reaches 30 VP (`stg_victory_vp`).

Tool note: `node scripts/battle_report/passives.cjs <name>` prints nothing. The file only exports
`passivesRecord` and has no CLI entry point. I called it through a wrapper,
`cl-day1/work-m07/passives_run.cjs`. That record also leaves out several identity rules:
Stalking Predation, Bestial Reflex, Profane Zeal, the Slave To Cupidity rerolls and the Otherworld
Grace evasions. I counted those from the log instead.

## Cross-battle summary

1. **Units walk off objectives they hold, and nothing in the AI weighs the VP they give up.**
   - M07 R2: Muqatila Troopers 2 fell back off the centre (rated "costly").
   - M08 R3: both Bailiffs crusader pairs walked off flanks they held unopposed, worth 4 VP that round.
   - M09 R4: Knight-Ascendants charged off the left objective after a Barghest that then fell back.
   - M10 R3: Azat Lancers 1 made a "doubtful" Fall Back and Andranik walked away, leaving the centre empty.
   - M10 R2-R4: **Fading Step** (the Sidhe identity's free move after being hit) carried A units out
     of the centre three times. That cost A the centre in R3 and R4 and handed it to B in R4.
   - M11 R2: two Etxe squads fell back off the centre.
   - M12 R3: three "doubtful" Fall Backs, and Knight of Tehom 1 died on its test.
2. **Last-round play repeats the M01 Hellkin pattern.**
   - M07 R5: the Weretiger Stalkers charged off the centre in the final round, and the Atavistic
     Rage it gained set its OC to 0. A then took the centre 6 vs 5, a 3 VP swing each way.
   - M11 R6: Hellkin made a "doubtful" Fall Back off the centre again and B kept it 4 vs 3.
   - M07 R5: Muqatila Troopers 2 left the centre on a Fall Back that needed a 6.
   - M09 R5: Knights of Camelot needed exactly 6 VP to win, yet spent 7 CP on a Troop Requisition and a
     Double Time that scored nothing.
3. **Magic is mostly unused, and one faction's Mana rule is missing.**
   - **Infernal Congregations' "Tithe of Torment" (1 Mana per HP a non-DEMON unit loses) is not
     implemented.** `magic/infernal_congregations/core_system.json` has no `effects` block and nothing
     in `engine/` names it. M08, M11 and M12 gained no Mana from damage; the only Infernal Mana came
     from Channel Mana, 2 in M08.
   - M07: the Wolfshead Pack gained 35 Mana and spent 6, ending with 29. Blessing of Sacred Rabies,
     Thicken the Hide and Forest of Fangs were never cast. The Rammal Geomancer gathered 6 Mana and
     cast nothing.
   - M09: Merlin gathered 5 Mana and never cast; 4 Gatescrolls were never spent, so House of Glass
     Mana never started. The Sidhe army had 0 Mana all battle.
   - M10: Summer Rade cast only Feth Fiada, 4 times, all rated "nothing", plus one counterspell.
   - M11: the Sorgin died with 3 Mana; Adur Mana cannot be earned on a flat map.
   - M12: the High Admiral had 0 Mana all battle.
4. **Identity rules that never fire, or are played against the army.**
   - Bailiffs (M08): Key to the Vault was never used, so the Gold economy never started and Margin
     Call ran 3 times at X=0.
   - Al-Kimiya (M07): Gather Ingredients was never used, and tokens were spent at 2 every round, so
     no 3-token formula was ever bought.
   - Euskal Herria (M11): Anboto Fusillade and Adur cannot trigger on CROSSROADS, which has no elevation.
   - Profane Zeal (M08): the only DEMON, the Stygian Actuary, spent the battle walking to the corner
     standard and back.
   - Atavistic Rage (M07): its -1 OC per model zeroed the Lycanthropes on the right objective (2 VP lost in R4).
   - Vahagn's Quarry (M10): Name the Quarry moved the QUARRY to a unit nobody shot for 4 rounds.
5. **Support abilities with no follow-up.**
   - Called To Account and Debt Called Due (M08): no friendly attack used the mark. This is the
     user-flagged case, and it happened again with Gaze of the Abyss the same round.
   - Burdinola Forge (M11): put on the Basajaun Smith 3 times; it never attacked.
   - Feth Fiada (M10): 4 uses, all rated "nothing".
   - Matter Transmutation (M07): Naft Pitch on Saladin in R5 bought nothing.
6. **Commands.** Double Time "bought nothing" 19 times across the six battles (both sides), 39 CP:
   M07 6, M08 3, M09 2, M10 2, M11 3, M12 3. Other wasted commands:
   - M08: Secure Objective on a unit that then fell back off the objective.
   - M07: Double Time on the Weretiger Stalkers, which did not move.
   - M08 R1: COMMAND AUTHORITY kept while no unit could reach the enemy half before it expired.
   - M09 R4: 5 CP held with a unit in the enemy half and COMMAND AUTHORITY active; it expired unscored.
7. **Free objectives and doable missions left alone.**
   - M09: the right objective was empty for all 5 rounds. Neither army's Coblynau Sappers or other
     units went there. A even held TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET on it from R3.
   - M08 R5-R6: the left was empty and next to two Bailiffs pairs.
   - M11: WIDE SWEEP scored 0 in R1-R4. In R3 and R4, Mawkin 2 stood 2 squares from the centre
     when a unit had to be more than 2 away; one more square was worth 2 VP each round.
8. **Friendly fire** happened three times:
   - M07 R2: the Marrowstring Centaur fired into the centre melee and hit its own Wildling Acolytes for 4.
   - M10 R5: Parsawor bombs splashed onto their own Pahak Riflemen 2 and destroyed it.
   - M12 R4: Cultist Troopers fired into the centre melee and hit their own Cultist Troopers 3 for 5.
9. **Expensive units that sat idle.**
   - M07: Weretiger Stalkers, 5 idle rounds, 0 attacks.
   - M08: The Chairman of the Chamber, 70 RP, 3 no-op Margin Calls and 4 damage, then retreated to
     its baseline. The Fulminator Squad made 0 Action-Phase attacks.
   - M09: Knight-Ascendants, 0 attacks.
   - M10: Vishap Harpooners and Reedfire Champion, 5 idle rounds. Andranik, 0 attacks.
   - M11: Basajaun Smith, 0 attacks. The Plague Monarch, 0 attacks.
