# Champion League Day 1 - AI and identity audit, D1-M01 to D1-M06

Sources: `digest.cjs`, `digest.cjs --log` (report.json log), `effects.cjs --uses`, `fallback.cjs`, the passives
record (`passivesRecord`), the round-end snapshots, and the unit / faction / magic data under
`data/editions/fantasy/`. Every claim below quotes the record. "Probe" Fall Back entries (engagers never
adjacent) are ignored.

## Cross-battle summary

1. **Expensive units that never fought.** In every battle at least one 25-80 RP unit made no attack, or one
   attack, in the whole game:
   - M01: Javidan Immortals 1 and 2 (60 RP, `actions 0/0/0/0/0` each) and the Somniferous Engine (30 RP).
   - M02: the Equites Igniferi (0 attacks), Chiron Battle Tank 2 (it never fired its cannon or its repeater)
     and Odin's Einherjar (0 attacks).
   - M03: the Palatine Custos (80 RP, 0 attacks).
   - M04: the Enceladan Ferricants (0 attacks) and the Prydain Field Gun (1 shot).
   - M05: the Tietaja (0 actions in 5 rounds) and the Leshen Besieger (1 attack).
   - M06: Consecrated Hussars 2 (0 damage dealt).

   The melee cavalry (Equites, Custos, Hussars 2) moved next to enemies and did not charge.
2. **Walking off objectives, and objectives left empty.**
   - M01 R2: Hellkin left the 3 VP centre it held alone.
   - M02 R2: the left wing pulled back and left Legionnaire Squad 2 alone.
   - M04 R2: two Ferricant units fell back and left the T.A.L.O.S alone on the centre.
   - M05 R4: Veche Levy 3 left (13,7), which then went unscored for two rounds (4 VP).
   - M06 R3: both units on (1,7) left it (2 VP), and the Commonwealth Fusiliers 3 walked into the
     Knight-Apostles and died.
   - M03 R4: three units walked off the centre, which then held by OC 6 vs 5.
3. **Final-round play that keeps units alive instead of scoring.**
   - M01 R5: Hellkin fell back off (1,7). The weighing scored the objective at 0 (`"now": 0, "ifLeaves": 0`).
   - M03 R4: the Maphorion Nuns and the Myrrhbearers walked off the centre.
   - M04 R4: Briarean Ferricants (the army's best shooter) walked away and idled.
   - M06 R4: Consecrated Hussars 1, 36 damage in the game, fell back. Kosciuszko fell back onto an
     objective full of enemies and died.
4. **Fall Back tests with poor odds.** 9 tests needed a 6+ and 3 passed. A failed test still moves the unit
   but deals disorder damage. That damage destroyed the Praetorian Fireteam (M02 R4) and set up Kosciuszko's
   death (M06 R3-R4). It also cost two Ferricant units HP in M04 R2.
5. **Commands that bought nothing.**
   - Double Time: 18 of 35 uses across the six battles gave `0 out of reach without it`. Several of the
     others moved units off objectives or into danger: M01 Shieldbearers 1 moved backwards, M06 Fusiliers 3
     left (1,7) and M06 Kosciuszko ran to his death.
   - Troop Requisition in the final round: M01 A R5 (3 CP) and M06 B R4 (3 CP, plus 2 CP of Double Time).
     Neither unit did anything.
   - Secure Objective bought nothing three times: M01 R5, M05 R4 and M06 R3.
6. **Magic: resource banked and never spent, or a system that never ran.**
   - M01 Infernal: Tithe of Torment looks unimplemented (0 Mana in 5 rounds, despite about 60 HP lost by
     Cultists).
   - M03 and M06 Covenant: 18 Mana banked at the end of each battle. The only Antiphon the AI ever cast was
     Introit (6 casts). M06 cast it on its HQ. M03 recast it over itself.
   - M04 British Liberation Front: all 3 Gatescrolls were never spent, so the House of Glass Mana income was 0.
   - M05 Kalevan: 0 Mana all game. Raise Luonto was never used, because the Tietaja moved every round.
   - Armies with Mana or Pietas and no MAGIC_CASTER to use it: M03 B (Pietas Tier IV), M04 A, M06 A
     (15 Mana).
   - M02 Iron Imperium: Pietas was blocked by Fall Backs in R2 and R3. The Augur banked 4 Mana.
7. **Poor Miracle use (M06).** Retribution went on a 10 RP Covenant Levies unit (0 hits) and Fortitude on
   another Levies unit. Providence was used twice.
8. **Secondaries kept that the army could not do.** FORWARD PRESENCE was kept by M01 A, M02 A, M04 A and
   M05 A, and nobody ever went near the enemy HQ. Others: OMNIPRESENCE (M01 B, M04 A), FORWARD DEPLOYMENT
   (M04 A, whose whole army stood within 5 squares of its own HQ), and INTO THE FRAY (M03 B, whose cavalry
   never charged).
9. **TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET pick in Round 1.** With all three CROSSROADS objectives the same distance from
   each HQ and nobody on them yet, the AI pick (`lead*100 + far`, `missions/gauntlet_pick.js`) names (7,7).
   That is the 3 VP centre the holder reaches first. M02 B and M06 A completed the card in the same round
   (+3 VP each).
10. **Engine bugs the record shows.**
    - (a) **FREE-weapon Overwatch.** A FREE weapon fired in Overwatch is skipped (`skipped - FREE weapon already
      used this Battle Round`) and then also cannot fire in the Action Phase. The M01 Spahbed lost its
      Firebreath in R2, R4 and R5. Seen in 11 Overwatch reactions (15 skip lines) in M01-M04: Spahbed, Otto,
      Praetorians, Custos, Sappers and the Artificer-General.
    - (b) **Round-end secondaries run after "this Battle Round" modifiers expire.** In M05 R5 B completed DEEP
      REACH although the scoring lines show it controlling no objective. A Secure Objective had decided (1,7).
      `action_cleanup.js` runs scoreObjectives before Modifiers.expireUnit, and `battle_flow.js:286` runs
      checkEndOfRoundMissions after both.
    - (c) Infernal Tithe of Torment has no implementation (see 6).
11. **Record and tool notes.**
    - The effects tool credits the Cultists' Desperate Worship to its target (`Desperate Worship by
      Shieldbearers 2`).
    - It prices Introit's Response and Mint Privilege's CP as "nothing".
    - Celeritas Squadron (M03) declares no subfactionId although its units are Officia Caesaris. It therefore
      ran Tacticas, and Mandata Principum never applied.
