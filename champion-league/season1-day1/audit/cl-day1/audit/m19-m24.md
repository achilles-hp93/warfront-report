# Champion League Day 1 - AI and identity audit, D1-M19 to D1-M24

Sources: `digest.cjs <name> --log`, `effects.cjs --uses`, `fallback.cjs`, `passivesRecord` (run on report.json),
report.json snapshots, moves, combats and resources, and unit, faction, mission and magic data under `data/`.
Quotes are copied from the digest or the log. "[in notes]" marks an item the Day notes page already lists; every
other item is new.
CROSSROADS geometry: centre (7,7) area = cols 6-8, rows 6-8; left (1,7) = cols 0-2, rows 6-8; right (13,7) =
cols 12-14, rows 6-8. A's enemy half is rows 0-6 and B's is rows 8-14 (`rowInEnemyHalf`); row 7 belongs to nobody.
The battle ends the moment a side has 30 VP (`stg_victory_vp`). If both sides reach it in the same round, the higher
total wins, and a tie goes to Player A (`checkVictoryConditions`). Tied OC goes to the side with more units in the
area, and a joined CHARACTER counts as a unit of its own (`objectiveControllerFromSummary`).
**DEEP REACH on CROSSROADS:** both HQs are exactly 6 squares (Chebyshev) from all three objectives, so DEEP REACH
means "control any objective" unless an HQ moves.

## Cross-battle summary (patterns)

1. **In the round that decides the battle, units give up VP that was there to take.** The record's own numbers
   show at least three results the AI could have changed.
   - **M22 (lost 25-33):** in Round 6, A spent its CP on a Double Time for an engaged unit and a Rally Cry,
     instead of a Secure Objective on the Levies holding the right objective. That objective then went to B on a
     6-6 tie. Holding it: A 30, B 28, and A wins.
   - **M24 (lost 28-31):** in Round 6, Praetorian Strikers 1 and Tribune 1 charged off the left objective they had
     held for three rounds (2 VP). In Round 5, Shield Wall's -1 MOVE left the Legionnaires exactly 5 squares from
     their HQ, which cost FORWARD DEPLOYMENT. With both: 31-31, which is a win for Player A.
   - **M19:** in Round 4, Saladin and Khassakiyya Guard 1 left the right objective. Staying would have left B on
     29 and taken the battle to Round 5.
   - **M20:** in Round 4, all four Popinjay Levies retreated to their home row while B held the centre on OC 7 vs
     0. Two of them in the area would have left B on 29.
   - Others: Levies 3's Fall Back off the centre (M22 Round 6), the Knight-Apostles' Fall Back off the centre (M21
     Round 4), and the Goetic Ophanim channelling Mana in the final round with 3 Mana banked (M23).
2. **Free objectives in reach are not taken; units stop one square or one row short.**
   - M19 Round 1: both Horns pairs had open paths of 5 squares (6-7 Movement Points) to the empty flank
     objectives. The Akritic Outriders stopped at (11,7), one square outside the right objective's area.
   - M20 Round 1: Silver Arrow Brethren 2 could reach the empty left objective with the Double Time it was given.
   - M22 Round 1: Muqatila Troopers 2 stopped at (1,5).
   - M23 Round 3: Commandery Bannermen 3 stopped at (13,5) next to an empty right objective.
   - M24 Rounds 1-2: Cain's pair and the Cantor-Preceptor each stopped one row short of an Objective Area, so LEAD
     FROM THE FRONT scored 0 in two rounds and B scored 0 VP in Round 2.
3. **CP is spent where it does nothing, and withheld where it would win an objective.**
   - Double Time "bought nothing" 21 times in these six battles (effects tool): M22 Soudarion 5 of 5 for 10 CP,
     M21 Flamewrought 3 of 3. Several went to engaged units or to units that then Fell Back.
   - Troop Requisition dropped units on the home row in the final round (M20 Round 4, M21 Round 4, M23 Round 5),
     and in M19 Round 3 bought Muqatila that were shot dead before acting (7 CP with the Double Time).
   - Secure Objective was not used where it flipped an objective: M19 Rounds 2-3, centre lost 11 vs 12 twice;
     M22 Round 6, the right objective, which decided the battle.
   - COMMAND AUTHORITY (M19 B) expired although Crimson Knights 2 and the Akritic Outriders stood in the enemy half
     at both Command Phases in its window. The HQ Commands went to units in B's own half or on row 7, the same
     pattern as the M08 example.
4. **Secondary choices and play.**
   - M19 A kept TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET over DEEP REACH while it held the right objective. DEEP REACH paid on any
     objective; the Gauntlet objective B named held OC 16 vs 0.
   - Missions kept that the army could not do: OMNIPRESENCE (M22 A, M24 B), BAR THE GATES against flyers (M22 A),
     PROBING STRIKE with no unit in the enemy half (M20 A), COMMAND AUTHORITY in Round 1 (M21 B).
   - FORWARD DEPLOYMENT failed by exactly one square twice: M19 (Khassakiyya Guard 2) and M24 (the Legionnaires).
5. **Covenant Miracles are spent by Round 2 or 3 on the cheapest units.**
   - Retribution: 6 uses across M19-M24 (including the one Cain's Patriarch of the Covenant restored), all on
     Levies, Silver Arrow daggers or Jordanite maces. Total damage: 1 HP.
   - Benediction healed 1 HP in three battles (M20, M21, M24). In M22 it healed 2 HP on a unit that died the next
     round.
   - Fortitude and Exaltation went on 10-13 RP targets.
   - Reprieve saved a 21 RP Cantor-Preceptor who died again to the next attack in the same phase (M24).
6. **Magic resources pile up unspent.**
   - Covenant PRAYING Mana with no caster in the army: Cappadocian Riders 14, Popinjay Guild 27, Belfry Battery 16
     [Belfry in notes].
   - Covenant armies with casters spent almost none: M22 used 4 of 30 Mana, and one of its two Introits went on the
     HQ [HQ walk in notes]; M24 used 4 of 28, and its second Introit re-cast the same Antiphon on the same unit for
     "no change".
   - Iron Imperium Pietas reached Tier IV (M20) and Tier V (M24) in two armies with no caster.
   - Atashid: Radiance gave 6 Mana after the only caster (the Mobad) died in Round 2 without casting.
   - **Infernal Tithe of Torment generated 0 Mana from 59 HP lost by non-DEMON units (M23).** The core system file
     has no effect and no engine code reads it, so it looks unimplemented (the M13-M18 audit found the same in M14).
7. **Caliphate Matter Transmutation goes on weapons that never attack.**
   - M19: 5 of 7 ingredients went on Khassakiyya Guard 1's Qilij Sabre. That unit fought only with its Rumh Lances
     and carbines.
   - M22: 7 of 8 ingredients went on Muqatila Troopers 1's Khanjar knife. That unit attacked once in melee in six
     rounds.
   - Every formula is priced "+0" except Red Sulfur (+0.52).
   - Gather Ingredients was used once in 10 Caliphate battle rounds, although units sat idle in four rounds with it
     as their best option at 0.5.
8. **Shooters lose their actions to movement.**
   - M21 Round 1: the Thunder Tower, both Field Guns and the Master Gunner Advanced, which bars acting, so they
     had "no legal action".
   - M20: the Pierced Laurel army moved before nearly every shot, so Stand Fast in Faith (reroll HIT 1s if not
     moved) never fired. Sniper Hesychast 2 never shot in 4 rounds. Field Gun 1 never fired.
   - Units fired at the enemy HQ, or tried to Capture it, which is worth nothing in Seize the Ground (M21
     Ardashir, M22 Field Gun, M24 Tribune 1).
9. **Rules that look broken.**
   - Introit's +2 MOVE made the MOVE-0 HQ walk three times (M22) [in notes]. That also changed which objective
     counted as farthest for DEEP REACH.
   - Visage of Superbia "survives at 1 HP and takes no damage for the rest of the Battle Round", but the Ashen
     Chirurgeon was killed by the rest of the same volley (M23).
   - The Dawn King (HOVER, OC 4/0) never landed, so it held OC 0 on the objectives it stood on for five rounds.
   - Qi-Reinforced Clay (REGENERATION 5+ in Formation) never rolled although the Terracotta Vanguard was wounded
     from Round 3.
   - Passage by Fire leaves no log line [in notes].
10. **Fall Back tests at 6+: 15 in six battles, 2 passed.** M20 Round 2 alone had four, all failed, by units that
    left DEBT OF BLOOD's marked target at 5 HP.
