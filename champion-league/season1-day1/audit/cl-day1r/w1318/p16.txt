 AI vs AI · After-action report
 March of the Restless Dead versus Golden Tumen 
 The Golden Tumen beat the March of the Restless Dead 30-5 in Battle Round 3, scoring the 10 VP maximum in every round and taking the target as fast as the cap allows. The Tamga-Bound Troops outnumbered the dead on every objective, and the Duat's one elite squad, the Akhguard, died attacking Genghis Khan without landing a blow.

 Deployment
 Central Faceoff on CROSSROADS, objectives at the centre (7,7), the west (1,7) and the east (13,7). The March of the Restless Dead lined up on rows 12 and 13: the Sphinx Construct and the Akhguard with the Canopic Priest on the west at (3,13) and (3,12), Ramesses, the Eternal Pharaoh and Skeletal Spearman 1 at (5,13) and (5,12), then Skeletal Archer 1, Skeletal Spearman 2, Skeletal Archer 3 and Skeletal Archer 2 from (8,12) to (11,12), and the Firelake Warden at (11,13). The Golden Tumen put four Tamga-Bound Troops squads in the middle of row 2, the Stormrider with the Black Shaman at (4,1), Khesig Guards 1 with Genghis Khan at (9,2), Khesig Guards 2 with the Stormlord at (11,2) and the Nerge Outriders at (11,1). With fewer units, the Tumen had Priority every round.
 Both sides had cheap mass, but the Tumen's was 20 HP and 10 OC a squad against the skeletons' 10 HP, and its CHARACTERs could reach the flanks in one move.

 Commentary
 Command The Duat's 2 CP Double Time on Skeletal Archer 3 bought nothing. The Tumen spent nothing and held 5 CP.
 Movement Tamga-Bound Troops 1 and 4 took the centre at (6,6) and (6,7); Khesig Guards 2 and the Stormlord reached the east at (12,7); Genghis Khan and Khesig Guards 1 came west to (3,5). Ramesses rolled 7 Movement Points and walked into the centre alone at (8,7), with Skeletal Spearman 2 beside him at (8,8); the Akhguard and the Canopic Priest took the west at (0,8).
 Action The Tumen shot first. Khesig Guards 2 and the Nerge Outriders took Ramesses from 10 HP to 5; the Stormrider hit the Akhguard for 2 and used Ride-by Fire to step to (2,7). The Firelake Warden's Soulfire Cannon put 4 into Tamga-Bound Troops 4.
 Cleanup The Tumen held the centre (OC 18 vs 15) and the east (OC 8 vs 0): 5 VP, LEAD FROM THE FRONT 3 for two CHARACTER groups forward, and IRON GRIP 2 and 1 CP. The Akhguard held the west (OC 9 vs 6) for 2 and STRONGPOINT paid 1 for Ramesses and Skeletal Spearman 2 in the centre area. 3-10.

 Commentary
 Command Both sides bought a unit with Troop Requisition (3 CP each): a Tamga-Bound Troops squad at (5,2) for the Tumen, a Skeletal Archer squad at (8,12) for the Duat. The Tumen added Secure Objective (3 CP) on Tamga-Bound Troops 1, which bought nothing this round. The Duat's 2 CP Double Time took Skeletal Archer 2 to (14,8), on the east edge.
 Movement All three Skeletal Archer squads fired Overwatch at the Nerge Outriders and missed; the Tamga-Bound Troops' Overwatch took 3 HP off Skeletal Archer 1 and 4 off Skeletal Archer 3. Skeletal Spearman 1 charged the Stormrider and the Black Shaman from (1,8), and the Stormrider broke away in disorder for 3 True Damage. Then Genghis Khan and Khesig Guards 1 charged into the west objective at (1,7), onto the Akhguard, the Canopic Priest and Skeletal Spearman 1. Khesig Guards 2 charged Skeletal Archer 2 at (13,8).
 Action The Black Shaman's Chant of the Black Tngri (3 Mana) took 1 MS and 1 BS off the Akhguard; then the Akhguard attacked Genghis Khan. Genghis struck first at INI 10 with his charge bonus for 7 damage, Khesig Guards 1 did the last 6, and the Akhguard died without a swing, leaving the Canopic Priest PARALYZED. The Canopic Priest's Endless Procession (3 Mana) raised Skeletal Archer #2 at (4,10). Skeletal Spearman 1's 10 dice against Genghis all missed. The Firelake Warden did 8 to Tamga-Bound Troops 1. The Nerge Outriders' Spot & Mark made Skeletal Spearman 2 MARKED, and three Tumen units shooting it completed FOCUSED FIRE for 2 VP and 1 CP.
 Cleanup The Tumen held all three objectives: the centre (OC 23 vs 14), the west (OC 26 vs 11) and the east (OC 8 vs 5). That is 7 VP, FOCUSED FIRE 2 and LEAD FROM THE FRONT 1, with 2 more withheld by the cap. The Duat scored nothing. 3-20.

 Commentary
 Command The Tumen spent 2 CP on Double Time to send the requisitioned Tamga-Bound Troops to the east at (12,6), and 3 CP on another Secure Objective for Tamga-Bound Troops 1, which this time decided the centre. The Duat's 2 CP Double Time on Skeletal Archer 1 bought nothing.
 Movement Khesig Guards 2 fell back to (14,6) under a 1 CP Go to Ground. The Canopic Priest failed its Fall Back test and broke away in disorder for 3 True Damage. Skeletal Archer #2 charged Genghis Khan at (2,8), and the Sphinx Construct charged the Nerge Outriders at (5,10).
 Action The Duat finally focused: the requisitioned Skeletal Archer, Skeletal Archer 3 and Skeletal Archer 1 took Tamga-Bound Troops 3 from 10 HP to 1, completing FOCUSED FIRE for 2 VP and 1 CP, and BURNED from Ramesses finished it. But the Tumen answered in kind: Khesig Guards 2 and Tamga-Bound Troops 1 took Ramesses to 2 HP, and the requisitioned Tamga-Bound Troops destroyed Skeletal Spearman 2. The Black Shaman's Chant of the Kindling (2 Mana) gave Tamga-Bound Troops 4 +1 DMG.
 Cleanup The Duat lost all three objectives by small margins: the centre 15 to 14, the west 17 to 15, the east 15 to 6. The Tumen's 7 VP and LEAD FROM THE FRONT's 3 made another 10 and the target. One kill and one loss each meant BLEED THEM WHITE paid the Duat nothing again. 5-30.

 The scoring record
 Who scored the VP. At every scoring moment the engine was asked what would have scored with some of the side's units absent: a unit's share is its extra VP averaged over every order its side's units could have arrived in (its Shapley value), so the shares add up to the VP scored. "Hinged on it" is the objective VP its side would have lost had that one unit not been there, the rest of the battle as it was.

 The AI on the Duat side
 The Akhguard is the army's one hard squad, and in Round 2 the AI attacked Genghis Khan and Khesig Guards 1 with it: both struck first at higher Initiative and it died without a swing, which lost the west objective and the only Duat unit that could win a melee. The Sphinx Construct (45 RP) did no damage and was idle two rounds of three. These look like AI choices rather than the army's limits; the skeletons' low OC and 10 HP squads are the army's.

 Against the first playing
 The first playing was a 36-13 Golden Tumen win in Round 4. Then the Akhguard won its fights, destroying Tamga-Bound Troops 2 and the Stormrider, and the Duat scored in every round. This time both armies were topped up (a third Skeletal Archer squad for the Duat, a fourth Tamga-Bound Troops squad for the Tumen) and both EPIC HEROes cost 10 RP less; the Akhguard spent its one attack on Genghis Khan and died. LEAD FROM THE FRONT now counts a CHARACTER and its joined squad as one, so its counts this time were 2 and 3 where the first playing's ran to 4, 5 and 6. The round cap held the Tumen to 10 a round, and it still finished a round sooner.
 Three perfect rounds
 The Golden Tumen did nothing spectacular: they put more OC on every objective than the dead could, kept their CHARACTERs forward, and let Genghis Khan punish the one Duat unit that came to fight him. The March of the Restless Dead needed its Akhguard to hold the west and threw it away in Round 2.
