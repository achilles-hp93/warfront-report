 Veiled Court versus Walls of Uruk 
 The Veiled Court beat the Walls of Uruk 38-17 in Battle Round 5, holding all three objectives in each of the last three rounds while Uruk scored only its Primary. The wall of Temple Levy that was meant to hold the line came apart from inside: one squad seized for a round by Whispers of Forbidden Pleasure, a second seized the next round, and a third defected for good to Temptation of Earthly Delight.

 Deployment
 Central Faceoff on CROSSROADS, objectives at the centre (7,7), the west (1,7) and the east (13,7). The Veiled Court set ten units across rows 12 and 13: the Masque of Carnality and the Lector (in the air) to the west, Cultist Troopers 3 and 4 in the middle, Cultist Troopers 1 with The Veiled Queen, Cultist Troopers 2 with the Queen Consort and both Babylonian Assassin squads to the east. In the Command Phase it paid 3 CP for Troop Requisition and placed a fifth Cultist Troopers squad at (3,12). The Walls of Uruk lined row 2 with Temple Levy 1 and the Asipu Priest at (3,2), Gilgamesh with the Ashur Breachguard at (5,2), the Floodgate Sappers at (7,2), Temple Levy 2 and 3 at (9,2) and (11,2), and the Lamassu Bastion at (11,1). With fewer units, Uruk had Priority every round.
 Uruk's plan was mass: three 10-model Levy squads to hold ground, Gilgamesh and the Breachguard to kill. The Veiled Court's answer was to take that mass away rather than fight it.

 Commentary
 Command Uruk enthroned Marduk with Twin Thrones (the record says it bought nothing). The Veiled Court's Troop Requisition (3 CP) put a fresh Cultist Troopers squad at (3,12); it went on to score 2.5 VP.
 Movement The Floodgate Sappers rolled 6 Movement Points and reached the centre at (8,7). Temple Levy 2 took the east at (13,6); Gilgamesh and the Breachguard came to (6,6). For the Veiled Court, Babylonian Assassin 1 ran to (6,8), Babylonian Assassin 2 to (12,8), and the Masque of Carnality to (2,7), beside the west objective.
 Action The only shot of the round: the Ashur Breachguard put 6 hits into Babylonian Assassin 1 and destroyed it. Tithe of Torment paid the Veiled Court 3 Mana for the 3 HP it lost.
 Cleanup Uruk held the centre (OC 11 vs 0) and the east (OC 10 vs 3) for 5 VP and completed TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY for 3. The Masque held the west alone (OC 1 vs 0) for 2. 2-8.

 Commentary
 Command Uruk spent all 4 of its CP on Secure Objective for Temple Levy 2 at the east objective: it bought nothing, and by the end of the round that squad was fighting for the other side. The Veiled Court paid 2 CP for Double Time on Cultist Troopers 3, which took it to (13,8), a move out of reach without it. Uruk switched Twin Thrones to Ashur Enthroned for 1 Recension.
 Movement Temple Levy 2's Overwatch put 5 damage on Cultist Troopers 3 as it arrived. The Floodgate Sappers charged the requisitioned Cultist Troopers, which broke away to (4,9). Then the Veiled Court charged: the Masque into the Asipu Priest and Temple Levy 1 at (1,6), Babylonian Assassin 2 into Temple Levy 2 at (12,7).
 Action Babylonian Assassin 2 struck first at INI 10 and, with DUAL WIELD and SUSTAINED ATTACK, rolled 18 hits on Temple Levy 2: 18 damage, 20 HP down to 2, then POISONED down to 1. The Masque put 8 hits and 10 damage into Temple Levy 1 and took nothing back. The Lector cast Black Vortex of Dis (5 Mana) on (4,5); it lands at the start of the next Action Phase, by which time nobody stood there. The Floodgate Sappers dug a Hiritu Ditch at (7,8). Last, The Veiled Queen used Whispers of Forbidden Pleasure on what was left of Temple Levy 2: seized until the end of the next Command Phase.
 Cleanup The seized Temple Levy 2 counted for the Veiled Court at the east (OC 13 vs 0), along with Cultist Troopers 3, which completed IRON GRIP (7 OC on one objective) for 2 VP and 1 CP. The Masque kept the west, the Sappers kept the centre (OC 3 vs 0) and survived as Uruk's FORLORN HOPE unit for 2. 8-13.

 Commentary
 Command At the start of the round Temptation of Earthly Delight claimed Temple Levy 1: the Asipu Priest left it for (1,4), and the squad defected to the Veiled Court. Temple Levy 2 went back to Uruk, failed its Morale Test and was SUPPRESSED. The Veiled Court spent 4 CP on Secure Objective for the Masque, which stood in Uruk's half: COMMAND AUTHORITY, 2 VP. Uruk's 2 CP Double Time on the Ashur Breachguard bought nothing.
 Movement Gilgamesh and the Breachguard charged Cultist Troopers 4 and the requisitioned Cultist Troopers at (4,8); the requisitioned squad failed its Fall Back test and broke away to (6,10) for 3 True Damage. Temple Levy 1, now the Veiled Court's, failed its Fall Back test from the Masque and broke away to (3,3), where the Lamassu Bastion charged its own former squad. The Sappers charged Cultist Troopers 4; the Masque charged Temple Levy 3 at (2,7).
 Action The Breachguard did 10 damage to Cultist Troopers 4 and Gilgamesh finished it with 3 hits. The Asipu Priest spent 5 Mana on Namburbi to counter the Lector's second Black Vortex of Dis. The Queen Consort's Gaze of the Abyss (2 Mana) took 1 MS and 1 BS off Temple Levy 3 just before the Masque hit it for 7. Cultist Troopers 2 shot the Floodgate Sappers dead (6 hits, 5 saves failed), and Temple Levy 2 attacked Babylonian Assassin 2, which struck first and finished it. The Veiled Queen then seized Temple Levy 3 with Whispers of Forbidden Pleasure.
 Cleanup The Veiled Court held all three objectives (OC 13, 2 and 11 against nothing) for 7 VP, plus COMMAND AUTHORITY and 1 VP of ERADICATE THE FOE: 10, with 2 VP of the Primary withheld by the VP cap. The Asipu Priest, named for FORLORN HOPE, survived for Uruk's 2. 18-15.

 Commentary
 Command The Veiled Court's 2 CP Double Time on Babylonian Assassin 2 bought nothing; the move was in reach anyway.
 Movement Temple Levy 3, back under Uruk's control, broke away from the Masque in disorder to (4,10). Babylonian Assassin 2 charged into the centre at (7,7); the Masque followed Temple Levy 3 to (3,9).
 Action The Ashur Breachguard shot Babylonian Assassin 2 dead through a 1 CP Go to Ground. The Masque rolled 6 hits on Temple Levy 3 for 11 damage and destroyed it; the Lamassu Bastion destroyed Temple Levy 1, now a Veiled Court unit. Then the Cultists turned on the Breachguard: Cultist Troopers 2 took 3 HP, Cultist Troopers 1 the last 5, inside the centre Objective Area, for KING OF THE HILL and 3 VP. The requisitioned Cultist Troopers, The Veiled Queen and the Queen Consort's Dark Bolt (3 Mana) took Gilgamesh from 10 HP to 1. The Asipu Priest's Adversary in Clay (4 Mana) put JINXED on the Lector and bought nothing.
 Cleanup All three objectives again for 7, KING OF THE HILL for 3: 10, and ERADICATE THE FOE's 2 were withheld by the cap. FIRE FOR EFFECT expired unfinished: the Veiled Court never damaged a unit it held at Intel Level 2 or higher. The Asipu Priest survived for Uruk's FORLORN HOPE once more. 28-17.

 The new 10 VP round cap was the only brake: the Veiled Court scored exactly 10 in Rounds 3, 4 and 5 and had 6 VP withheld, so without it the battle would have ended in Round 4.
 The Levy wall came apart
 Uruk's ground-holding was three Temple Levy squads, and all three were taken away. Babylonian Assassin 2 did 18 of Temple Levy 2's 20 HP in one charge, and Whispers of Forbidden Pleasure seized the rest, so its OC counted for the Veiled Court at the east objective in Round 2. The scoring record shows who held the ground instead: Cultist Troopers 3 (10 RP, 8.7 VP), sitting on the east objective from Round 2, and the Masque of Carnality (8.0 VP, 6 objective VP that hinged on it), which held the west for three rounds and did 18 damage.
 What Uruk's fighters did
 The Ashur Breachguard was Uruk's best unit: 19 damage and both Babylonian Assassin squads killed. Gilgamesh (65 RP) did 6 damage, killed Cultist Troopers 4 and spent three rounds idle; the Lamassu Bastion (60 RP) did 12 damage and its one kill was a squad Uruk had started the battle with. With the Levy gone, nothing was left to stand on objectives.

 Against the first playing
 The first playing was a much closer Veiled Court win, 33-28 in Round 6. Uruk then completed ASSASSINATION in Round 2 (the Lector, to Overwatch), TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY in Round 3 and KING OF THE HILL in Round 5, and scored FORLORN HOPE four times. This time the Veiled Court fielded a fourth Cultist Troopers squad and its Veiled Queen cost 60 RP instead of 70, Babylonian Assassin 2 and the Masque won every melee they fought, and Whispers of Forbidden Pleasure and the defection took all three Levy squads out of Uruk's count by Round 4. Uruk's 28 became 17, and the battle was a round shorter.
 An army that takes its enemy's units
 The Walls of Uruk built to hold ground and lost the units that held it, not in a firefight but to seizure and defection. The Breachguard did its job; the rest of the army had nothing to stand on objectives once the Levy were gone, and the Veiled Court scored the maximum allowed for three rounds running.
