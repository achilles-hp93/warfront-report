 Winter Host versus Glandore Breakers 
 The Glandore Breakers beat the Winter Host 35-22 in Battle Round 6, after leading 17-2 at the end of Round 2. The Sidhe reached all three objectives in the first move and banked that lead; the Winter Host fought back to win Rounds 4 and 5, but never held more than the west objective, and lost units to its own choices along the way.

 Deployment
 Central Faceoff on CROSSROADS, objectives at the centre (7,7), the west (1,7) and the east (13,7). The Winter Host deployed on row 12: the Rynda Guard with Rurik the Deathless at (3,12), the Firelance Druzhina at (4,12), Veche Levy 1, 2 and 3 at (5,12), (9,12) and (11,12), and the Bogatyr Thunderguards with the Winter Witch at (10,12). The Glandore Breakers put the Fomorian Tidebreaker at (3,1), Briton Yeomen 2 at (4,2), Sidhe Wardens 2 with the Fey Lord at (5,2), Sidhe Wardens 3 at (7,2), Sidhe Wardens 1 with Cliodhna - The Dread Tide at (9,2), Morrigan's Chosen at (10,2) and Briton Yeomen 1 at (11,2). With fewer units, the Winter Host had Priority in Rounds 1 to 4.
 The Sidhe move far, and the Veche Levy do not: the first move decided who stood on the objectives.

 Commentary
 Command The Winter Host's 2 CP Double Time on the Firelance Druzhina bought nothing. The Flow of Magic gave the Breakers Tree.

The Fomorian Tidebreaker charged the Firelance Druzhina at (2,6).

 Cleanup The Breakers held all three: the centre (OC 25 vs 0), the west (OC 13 vs 4) and the east (OC 10 vs 0), 7 VP. With no Breakers unit in its half, the Winter Host completed BAR THE GATES for 2. 2-7.

A CHARGE or a Fall Back is a fight's move and does not count. In this battle 7 of 58 moves were undone.

 Speed, then attrition
 The Breakers won the battle in its first move: the Sidhe stood on every objective at the end of Round 1 while the Veche Levy were still two rows short. After that the Winter Host traded well.  What the Winter Host gave away
 Three of its losses look like AI choices. In Round 2 the Firelance Druzhina attacked Sidhe Wardens 1 and Cliodhna, who strike first at INI 11 and 8 with their charge bonus, and died without a swing; in Round 4 the Bogatyr Thunderguards, on 5 HP, attacked the Fomorian Tidebreaker, which also struck first, and died the same way. In Round 3 the Bogatyr Thunderguards fired into the melee between the Fomorian and Veche Levy 2; the shot strayed, did 11 damage to Veche Levy 2, and the Fomorian finished it. In Round 6, the last round, the Rynda Guard and Rurik left the west objective to charge Briton Yeomen 2: the Winter Host scored no objective and handed the Breakers BREAK THEIR HOLD's 3 VP, though the Breakers would have reached 30 without them. The Breakers' AI wasted less: a Secure Objective and a Shapeshifter in Round 2 that bought nothing.

 Against the first playing
 The first playing was a 31-14 Breakers win in Round 4, when they scored 13 VP in one round to finish. This time the 10 VP cap held them to 10 in Round 2 and Round 6, and the Winter Host won Round 4 9-1 and Round 5 4-3, so the battle went two rounds longer and the Winter Host scored 22 instead of 14. Rurik the Deathless and Cliodhna each cost 10 RP less, and two Veche Levy and two Sidhe Wardens squads were topped up by 5 RP. In the first playing the Fomorian Tidebreaker died in its own failed Fall Back; this time it was shot dead in the east Objective Area and counted for ERADICATE THE FOE.
 Fast first, then just enough
 The Glandore Breakers won on the first two rounds and spent the rest of the battle holding on. The Winter Host had the better middle of the battle and the units to win a slower game; it lost the race to the objectives at the start and three units to fights it should not have picked.
