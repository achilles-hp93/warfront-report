 Mouseion Contingent versus Protectores Divini Lateris 
 Mouseion Contingent won 32-28 in Battle Round 4, reaching the 30 VP target one round before the Protectores Divini Lateris could catch them. The Panhellenic League banked 17 VP in two rounds from three objectives and a run of easy secondaries, and the Palatine Custos only started killing in Round 3, too late to turn a fight they won into a game they won.

 Deployment
 Central Faceoff on CROSSROADS, with three objectives across the middle row: the centre at (7,7), the west at (1,7) and the east at (13,7). The Mouseion Contingent put nine units along rows 12 and 13, spread over the whole width: Argyraspides 2 with the Asklepian Medic and the Hephaestian Tripod to the west, Argyraspides 1 with Heron of Alexandria and both League Hoplite squads to the east, the Ikarus Skirmishers in the air at (7,12) and the Heliostat Battery at (3,13). The Protectores Divini Lateris brought five units on row 2: Palatine Custos 1 with the Palatine Protospatharius at (3,2), the Legionnaire Squad at (5,2), Palatine Custos 2 at (9,2) and the Palatine Artillerists at (11,2). Fewer units gave the Iron Imperium Priority every round.
 The spread mattered more than the quality. The Panhellenic League stood on two objectives after one move and on all three after two; the Protectores had nobody aimed at the east, and never sent anyone there.

 Commentary
 Command The Protectores spent 1 CP on a Scouting Order for Palatine Custos 2 (+2 PER); the record says it bought nothing. Mouseion kept TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET, and the Protectores chose the west objective at (1,7) for it.
 Movement Everyone advanced. Delphic Apotropaia spent an Omen Die of 5 for +1 MOVE to every Panhellenic League unit. League Hoplite 1 and Argyraspides 1 reached the centre (8,8) and (7,8); Argyraspides 2 and the Medic reached (2,8), right beside the west objective; the Legionnaire Squad answered at (1,6). Nobody had a shot in the Action Phase.
 Cleanup Mouseion took the centre 3 VP (OC 23 vs 8) and the west 2 VP by one point of OC (11 vs 10), so the Gauntlet paid 3 more. The Protectores' BREAK THEIR HOLD paid 2 for standing in both contested areas. 8-2.

 Commentary
 Command The Protectores spent all 3 of their CP on Double Time for the Legionnaire Squad. The range surcharge made it 3 CP, not 2, and the squad then moved one square, from (1,6) to (0,6): it bought nothing. Mouseion paid 2 CP for Double Time on League Hoplite 2, which took it to (1,8), a move it could not have made without the command.
 Movement Both Custos squads charged Heron of Alexandria and Argyraspides 1, Palatine Custos 2 from (6,7) and Palatine Custos 1 with the Protospatharius from (6,8). Argyraspides 1 failed its Fall Back test (needed 6+, rolled 3) and broke away in disorder to (10,9) for 2 True Damage, so the charges found nothing to fight. Overwatch went both ways: Argyraspides 2 put 2 damage on the Palatine Artillerists, the Legionnaires 2 on League Hoplite 2, and League Hoplite 2 4 on the Legionnaires. Then Argyraspides 2 charged the Legionnaire Squad.
 Action The Artillerists used Exploding Shell (SWEEP 2) on League Hoplite 1. Mouseion answered with Go to Ground (1 CP), but the squad still took 4; the Protospatharius, activated straight after by Mandata Principum, added 1 with the Helios Pistol and left it BURNED for 2 more at the end of the phase. Argyraspides 2 rolled 7 hits on the Legionnaires and destroyed them, the Protectores' only loss of the battle. The Hoplites put 4 damage into Palatine Custos 2.
 Cleanup The Custos took the centre (OC 20 vs 4), which turned BREAK THEIR HOLD into its 3 VP form and completed DEEP REACH for 3 more. Mouseion held the west (OC 19 vs 0) and the east, where the Hephaestian Tripod stood alone (OC 1 vs 0), so TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY paid 3, and League Hoplite 1, the unit the Protectores picked for FORLORN HOPE, was alive outside its zone for 2. 17-11.

 Commentary
 Command The Protectores paid 2 CP for Double Time on the Palatine Artillerists, which took them from (8,6) to (3,7), toward the west objective. Mouseion spent 2 CP on Rally Cry for League Hoplite 1, which passed its Morale Test.
 Movement Argyraspides 1 and Heron of Alexandria walked back toward the centre at (8,7), into the Custos' FREE Volcanus Pistols: both squads fired them in Overwatch, for 2 and 3 damage. Then both Custos squads charged again. League Hoplite 1 failed its Fall Back test and broke away to (10,6) with 1 model lost; Argyraspides 1 and Heron stayed.
 Action Palatine Custos 1 struck first at INI 9 and destroyed Argyraspides 1, 9 damage from 4 hits. Mandata Principum activated Palatine Custos 2 straight after, and with its bodyguard gone Heron of Alexandria took 4 hits and 7 damage and died: ASSASSINATION, 4 VP. The Artillerists put 4 into League Hoplite 2; the League's own fire on the Artillerists was all saved.
 Cleanup The Protectores held the centre for 3 VP but had no unit in either objective Mouseion held, so BREAK THEIR HOLD paid nothing. Mouseion held the west with League Hoplite 2 (OC 4 vs 0) and the east with the Tripod, completed DEEP REACH for 3, and League Hoplite 1, on 1 HP, scored FORLORN HOPE again. 26-18.

 Commentary
 Command Mouseion held 3 CP and spent none. League Hoplite 1 failed a Morale Test on a natural 1 and was SUPPRESSED.
 Movement The Artillerists stepped from (3,7) into the west objective at (2,8), completing OBJECTIVE RELAY for 2 VP and 1 CP. Palatine Custos 1 came across to (4,7); the Protospatharius bought Go to Ground (1 CP) for it against the Overwatch of Argyraspides 2, which hit twice; both were saved.
 Action The Artillerists' second Exploding Shell rolled 8 hits from 9 dice on Argyraspides 2, and only 1 of 8 saves held: 15 damage to the squad and 5 to the Asklepian Medic, whose two Feel No Pain rolls failed. Palatine Custos 1, activated straight after, finished League Hoplite 2 with its pistols. The west objective was now the Protectores'.
 Cleanup The Protectores took the centre and the west for 5 VP, BREAK THEIR HOLD paid 3 for the west changing hands, and OBJECTIVE RELAY added 2: exactly 10, the most a player can score in a round. Mouseion still scored 6: the Tripod held the east for 2 and completed SECURE THE FLANKS for 2, and League Hoplite 1 survived on 1 HP for its third FORLORN HOPE. 32-28 , and the target was 30.

 The scoring record
 Who scored the VP. At every scoring moment the engine was asked what would have scored with some of the side's units absent: a unit's share is its extra VP averaged over every order its side's units could have arrived in (its Shapley value), so the shares add up to the VP scored. "Hinged on it" is the objective VP its side would have lost had that one unit not been there, the rest of the battle as it was. VP no unit decided (an objective held by capture, a no-losses card): Protectores Divini Lateris 3 VP.

 Why it went this way
 Where the VP came from
 Mouseion's 32 came from objectives (15), secondaries (11: TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET, TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY and DEEP REACH at 3 each, SECURE THE FLANKS at 2) and FORLORN HOPE (6). The Protectores' 28 split into objectives (11), secondaries (9: DEEP REACH 3, ASSASSINATION 4, OBJECTIVE RELAY 2, with STRONGPOINT still open) and BREAK THEIR HOLD (8). Both sides completed a secondary nearly every round, so the gap was objectives: Mouseion scored at least one objective in all four rounds and held the east every round from Round 2, the Protectores held only the centre until Round 4.

 Two cheap units won it
 The scoring record gives the Hephaestian Tripod 10.8 VP, the most of any unit, for 20 RP: all 6 objective VP at the east objective hinged on it, and it carried TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY and SECURE THE FLANKS. It spent no APt all game (no legal action each round), which is the record of a unit doing exactly its job by standing still. League Hoplite 1 (7.5 VP) was the unit the Protectores named for FORLORN HOPE three rounds running, and it survived each time, the last two on a single HP after BURNED and a disorderly Fall Back.
 The Protectores won the fight
 The Iron Imperium destroyed 160 RP (Heron of Alexandria, both Argyraspides squads, the Asklepian Medic, League Hoplite 2) and lost 20, the Legionnaire Squad. The Palatine Artillerists did 28 damage with 2 kills, mostly through two Exploding Shells, and the two Custos squads killed Argyraspides 1 and Heron in one Mandata Principum chain. But the killing came in Rounds 3 and 4, after Mouseion had built a lead the 10 VP round cap kept them from closing: their Round 4 was a perfect 10 and still finished 2 short of the target.

 What the loser could not do, and what was the AI
 No Protectores unit went east all game, so the Tripod's 2 VP a round, and BREAK THEIR HOLD's 1 VP for that objective, were never contested. With five units against nine, that is partly the army: the Protectores cannot cover three objectives and fight in the middle. The rest was the AI. It spent 3 CP, all it had in Round 2, on a Double Time for the Legionnaire Squad that moved one square and bought nothing, and a Scouting Order in Round 1 that also bought nothing. On the other side, the Heliostat Battery (34 RP) moved to the corner at (0,13) in Round 1 and had no legal action in any of the four rounds; its only shot was one missed Overwatch. Mouseion won with its gun idle for four rounds.

 Against the first playing
 The first playing was also a Mouseion win in Round 4, 32-20, with only one unit destroyed all game (the Ikarus Skirmishers) and both Palatine Custos squads idle in all four rounds. This time the Protectores fielded the new Legionnaire Squad, the Custos cost 60 RP each instead of 70, and the Custos fought: they charged in Rounds 2 and 3, fired their FREE Volcanus Pistols in Overwatch (5 damage in Round 3), and completed the ASSASSINATION the first playing kept and never finished. That, OBJECTIVE RELAY and the Round 4 sweep of the west took the Protectores from 20 to 28. Mouseion's 32 did not move.
 Early ground beats late violence
 The Protectores Divini Lateris played the better battle from Round 3 on and lost anyway: a 20 RP Tripod no one challenged and a Hoplite squad that would not die were worth more than four kills. With five units, the Iron Imperium needs to contest every objective from the first round, not the last.
