 AI vs AI · After-action report
 Bulava of the Rada versus Chonae Wing 
 The Chonae Wing beat the Bulava of the Rada 30-8 in Battle Round 4, holding the east in every round, the west from Round 2 and the centre in Rounds 2 and 4. The Cossacks lost their Primary in the first round, when the standard they planted at (4,6) was overrun, and their fast cavalry never found enough bodies to hold ground against six squads of Covenant Levies.

 Deployment
 Central Faceoff on CROSSROADS, objectives at the centre (7,7), the west (1,7) and the east (13,7). The Bulava of the Rada deployed eight units on row 13: Host Sotnia 2 with the Kharakternyk at (3,13), Host Sotnia 1 with the Host Osaul at (4,13), Terek Shashka Riders 2 at (6,13), Terek Shashka Riders 1 with Ivan Sirko (Mounted) at (9,13) and Host Sotnia 3 at (11,13). The Chonae Wing put six Covenant Levies squads along row 2 from (4,2) to (10,2), Michaelite Brethren 2 with the Cantor-Preceptor at (3,2), and the Saint-Militant (5,2) and Michaelite Brethren 1 with the Seraphic Justiciar (11,2) in the air. With fewer units, the Cossacks had Priority every round.
 For RAISE THE STANDARD the Cossacks planted their standard at (4,6), in the Chonae Wing's half, where it would pay 2 VP a round, and four squares in front of the Michaelite Brethren 2.

 Commentary
 Command Three commands bought nothing: the Cossacks' Scouting Order (1 CP) on Host Sotnia 3, and the Chonae Wing's Double Time (2 CP) on Covenant Levies 5 and Scouting Order (1 CP) on Michaelite Brethren 1.
 Movement The Cossacks rode for the centre: Host Sotnia 1 to (6,7), Terek Shashka Riders 1 and Ivan Sirko to (6,8), Terek Shashka Riders 2 to (6,6). The Levies met them, Covenant Levies 5 at (8,8) and Covenant Levies 1 at (8,6), with Covenant Levies 4 taking the east at (12,6). Michaelite Brethren 2 walked onto the Cossack standard at (4,6). No unit had a shot.
 Cleanup The centre was tied, OC 20 against 20 and three units each, so nobody scored it. Covenant Levies 4 held the east alone for 2 VP. With a Chonae Wing unit beside it and no Cossack within a square, the standard was torn down for good: the Cossacks' Primary was gone. PRAYING gave the Chonae Wing 14 Mana. 0-2.

 Commentary
 Movement Michaelite Brethren 2 fired its FREE weapons in Overwatch at Host Sotnia 2 for 3 damage through a 1 CP Go to Ground. Terek Shashka Riders 1 charged Covenant Levies 5 and 6 at (7,8); the Providence Miracle helped Covenant Levies 1's Overwatch hit Terek Shashka Riders 2 for 2. Then the flyers landed their charges: Michaelite Brethren 1 and the Seraphic Justiciar on Host Sotnia 2 and the Kharakternyk, the Saint-Militant on Host Sotnia 1 and the Host Osaul.
 Action Terek Shashka Riders 1 did 10 to Covenant Levies 6 and Ivan Sirko finished it with 4 hits; the Retribution Miracle let the Levies fight before they were removed, for no hits. With Exaltation, the Saint-Militant rolled 6 hits and did 15 damage to Host Sotnia 1, destroying it; the Host Osaul's counterattack did 6 and INSTRUMENT OF WRATH kept the Saint-Militant alive on 1 HP. The Cantor-Preceptor sang Agnus Dei (6 Mana) over Michaelite Brethren 1, which bought nothing. Host Sotnia 2 used Riders of the Wild Fields to slip out to (8,10). Covenant Levies 1 then shot into the melee around Terek Shashka Riders 1 and the shot strayed into Covenant Levies 5 for 6 damage.
 Cleanup The Chonae Wing held the centre (OC 17 vs 15), the west with Michaelite Brethren 2 (OC 7 vs 0) and the east: 7 VP. Host Sotnia 1 died in the centre Objective Area, so ERADICATE THE FOE paid 2, and DEEP REACH paid 1 of its 3, the rest withheld by the 10 VP cap. 0-12.

 Commentary
 Command The Benediction Miracle healed the Saint-Militant 3 HP. The Chonae Wing spent 3 CP on Secure Objective for Michaelite Brethren 2 in the Cossack half, completing COMMAND AUTHORITY for 2 VP, and 2 CP on Double Time for Covenant Levies 3 toward the centre. The Cossacks went into the round with 7 CP and spent 1, on Go to Ground for Host Sotnia 2.
 Movement The Host Osaul failed its Fall Back test from the Saint-Militant and broke away to (7,10) for 2 True Damage. The Saint-Militant charged Terek Shashka Riders 2, and Michaelite Brethren 1 landed on Host Sotnia 3 and Terek Shashka Riders 2.
 Action Terek Shashka Riders 1 cut down Covenant Levies 5, 14 damage from 8 hits, and took ground at (8,8). Terek Shashka Riders 2 then attacked the Saint-Militant, which had Agnus Dei on it and struck first at INI 9 with its charge bonus: 14 damage, and Michaelite Brethren 1 finished the Riders. Host Sotnia 3 and Ivan Sirko put 6 damage into Covenant Levies 1; Covenant Levies 1 and 3 put 9 into Host Sotnia 3.
 Cleanup The Cossacks took the centre (OC 11 vs 7) for 3, completed IRON GRIP for 2 and 1 CP, and DEEP REACH for 3: 8, their whole score for the battle. The Chonae Wing kept the west (OC 11 vs 5) and the east, and ERADICATE THE FOE paid 2 for Terek Shashka Riders 2. 8-20.

 Commentary
 Command With 10 CP the Cossacks bought a Troop Requisition (5 CP with the range surcharge), a fresh Host Sotnia at (6,12), and a 4 CP Secure Objective on Host Sotnia 2 that bought nothing. The Chonae Wing's 3 CP Secure Objective on Covenant Levies 4 bought nothing either. The Cossacks chose the east objective for the Chonae Wing's TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET.
 Movement Terek Shashka Riders 1 charged Covenant Levies 3 at (8,7). The Saint-Militant and Michaelite Brethren 2 both charged Host Sotnia 2 and the Kharakternyk at the west edge; Michaelite Brethren 1 and the Justiciar landed on Ivan Sirko, Terek Shashka Riders 1 and Host Sotnia 3.
 Action Terek Shashka Riders 1 did 14 to Covenant Levies 3 and Ivan Sirko finished it. Michaelite Brethren 2 rolled 6 hits on Host Sotnia 2 and destroyed the squad and the Kharakternyk together, which completed MAN OF THE HOUR for 4 VP and 1 CP. Host Sotnia 3, on 6 HP, attacked the Seraphic Justiciar, which struck first and killed it before it could swing. Covenant Levies 2, under Agnus Dei, shot the new Host Sotnia dead.
 Cleanup The Chonae Wing held all three objectives and reached 30 VP exactly: the centre (OC 16 vs 9) 3, the west (OC 17 vs 0) 2, the east 1 of its 2, and MAN OF THE HOUR 4. The cap withheld 7 VP: 1 from the east, 3 from ERADICATE THE FOE and all 3 of TAKE UP THE GAUNTLET. 8-30.

 RAISE THE STANDARD
 After choosing this card, plant your standard on a square at least 3 squares from your deployment zone and from every objective, outside the enemy deployment zone. At the end of each Battle Round, score 1 VP if a friendly unit is within 1 square of the standard and no enemy unit is, or 2 VP if the standard stands in the enemy half. Score 1 VP more if you also held it at the end of the previous Battle Round. If an enemy unit is within 1 square of it and no friendly unit is at the end of a Battle Round, it is torn down for good.

 What the Cossacks could not do, and what was the AI
 RAISE THE STANDARD was lost to placement. The AI planted the standard at (4,6), in the enemy half for the 2 VP rate, four squares in front of Michaelite Brethren 2, which walked onto it in the first Movement Phase; no Cossack ever got within a square of it. TERRITORIAL SUPERIORITY and PROBING STRIKE were never completed: the Cossacks never held more objectives than the Chonae Wing, and the round they kept PROBING STRIKE was the last. Two more AI choices cost them: they went into Round 3 holding 7 CP and spent 1, then spent 4 in Round 4 on a Secure Objective that bought nothing; and in Rounds 3 and 4 Terek Shashka Riders 2 and Host Sotnia 3 attacked units that struck first at higher Initiative, losing the first and dying without a swing in the second. The Chonae Wing had its own lapses (Covenant Levies 1 shooting into a melee and hitting its own Covenant Levies 5 for 6 HP, three commands that bought nothing in Round 1, an Agnus Dei on Michaelite Brethren 1 that changed nothing), but it had the bodies to absorb them.
 MAN OF THE HOUR asks one unit to destroy two enemy units. Michaelite Brethren 2's two kills were Host Sotnia 2 and the Kharakternyk leading it, in one attack. Under the rule that a joined CHARACTER and its squad count as one unit for every count, that should be one kill, and the card should still have been open. The 4 VP it paid did not change the result.
