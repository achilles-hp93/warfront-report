 AI vs AI · After-action report
 Murderhost of Halfa versus Kishomon Company 
 The Murderhost of Halfa beat the Kishomon Company 36-24 in Battle Round 6, holding the centre objective at the end of every round and the east in five of six. The Murderhost's Round 2 charge broke all three Ashigaru squads off their ground; Kishomon's only steady score was the west objective, held from Round 2 by an Armored Gashadokuro that never took an action.

 Deployment
 Central Faceoff on CROSSROADS, objectives at the centre (7,7), the west (1,7) and the east (13,7). The Murderhost of Halfa lined rows 12 and 13 with ten units: Cultist Zealots 3 and 2 and Hounds of Satan 1 on the west, the Cultist Troopers, Furyforged Berserkers 1 with The Blooded Hegemon and Cultist Zealots 1 in the middle, the Avatar of Wrath, Furyforged Berserkers 2 and Hounds of Satan 2 to the east. The Kishomon Company put the Kitsune Shinobi HIDDEN at (1,3), the Tsuchigumo Gun Battery at (3,1), the Armored Gashadokuro at (5,1), Ashigaru Riflemen 2, 3 and 1 (with the War Onmyoji) across row 2, the Oni Samurai with the Taichou at (11,2), and three Shikigami Tokens in the corner for the War Onmyoji's Paper Body. With fewer units, Kishomon had Priority every round.
 The Murderhost is a melee army with no ranged threat to speak of; Kishomon's plan was to hold ground with rifles and let the Oni Samurai and the gun do the killing.

 Commentary
 Command Kishomon spent 1 Regalia on YATA NO KAGAMI, marking five Murderhost units for +1 AP, and 3 CP (with the range surcharge) on Double Time for the Kitsune Shinobi, which bought nothing.
 Movement The Kitsune Shinobi moved first, to (6,6) beside the centre, and lost HIDDEN when Cultist Zealots 1 charged them at (7,7). Hounds of Satan 2 took the east at (13,7) with the Avatar of Wrath behind; the Cultist Troopers and Hounds of Satan 1 filled the centre. The Ashigaru stopped short at rows 5 and 6.
 Action The Tsuchigumo Gun Battery hit the Cultist Troopers for 6. Cultist Zealots 1 rolled 21 dice at the Kitsune Shinobi, got 7 hits and destroyed them, then took ground at (6,6).
 Cleanup The Murderhost held the centre (OC 12 vs 0) and the east (OC 7 vs 0) for 5 VP; Kishomon scored nothing. 5-0.

 Commentary
 Command Kishomon bought a fresh Ashigaru Riflemen squad with Troop Requisition (5 CP) at (7,2). The Murderhost's 2 CP Double Time on Cultist Zealots 1, in Kishomon's half, completed COMMAND AUTHORITY for 2 VP.

 Action The Oni Samurai destroyed Furyforged Berserkers 2. Cultist Zealots 1 caught the War Onmyoji again: Paper Body spent the last Shikigami Token, and the second blow killed it, for ASSASSINATION and 4 VP. The Tsuchigumo hit the Cultist Troopers for 4.

The cap withheld the other east VP and all 3 of BREAK THEIR HOLD. Because a Primary that earned VP its cap held back counts as scoring, Kishomon's STRATEGICAL DISRUPTION paid nothing; the Gashadokuro's west objective paid 2. 19-7.

 Moves undone
 A move the unit's next move undoes is a turn spent getting nowhere, as in chess when a piece goes and comes back while the other side develops. A pair counts when the second move takes the unit back beside where the first began, or away again from an objective the first moved toward, no nearer than it started. A CHARGE or a Fall Back is a fight's move and does not count. In this battle 0 of 65 moves were undone.

 The charge that won it
 The battle turned in Round 2. Eight Murderhost units charged, every Ashigaru squad failed its Fall Back test, and by the end of the round Ashigaru Riflemen 1 was gone and Ashigaru Riflemen 2 was down to 2 HP. KUSANAGI NO TSURUGI was Kishomon's best answer, 20 HP and six units PARALYZED, and the Oni Samurai (36 damage, 3 kills, the most on the table) kept killing for four rounds, but Kishomon never again had the bodies to contest the centre. Cultist Zealots 1 (10 RP) was the Murderhost's best unit: 31 damage, 4 kills, ASSASSINATION and COMMAND AUTHORITY.

 Kishomon's two engines
 Kishomon scored from two places. The Armored Gashadokuro (55 RP) stood in the west from Round 2 to the end and spent no APt in any of the six rounds, with no legal action each time, yet the scoring record gives it 17.5 VP, the most of any unit: all 10 west objective VP hinged on it, and it carried STRATEGICAL DISRUPTION and DEEP REACH. That is a holding unit doing its job; no Murderhost unit entered the west Objective Area after Round 3. The other was STRATEGICAL DISRUPTION, which paid 2 VP in every round the Murderhost's BREAK THEIR HOLD scored nothing. From Round 4 the Murderhost had no unit in an objective Kishomon controlled, so its Primary was dead and fed Kishomon's.
 AI notes
 Commands were the weak spot on both sides: Kishomon's Round 1 Double Time (3 CP) on the Kitsune Shinobi, which then died, and its Round 4 Double Time both bought nothing, as did the Murderhost's Round 2 Secure Objective (3 CP). The Murderhost never sent a unit to the west, which would have both contested Kishomon's only objective and revived BREAK THEIR HOLD. Its Mana grew to 51 from Tithe of Torment and was never spent: no unit in this preset casts. That is the army, not the AI.

The Murderhost added a Cultist Troopers squad and The Blooded Hegemon cost 60 RP instead of 70; Kishomon's Tsuchigumo Gun Battery dropped from 35 to 26 RP and its Oni Samurai and Kitsune Shinobi went up. Tithe of Torment, new since then, gave the Murderhost 51 Mana it had no use for.
 The centre, every round
 The Murderhost of Halfa did what a melee army has to: closed in Round 2, broke the rifle line and sat on the middle of the board. Kishomon's Regalia and Oni Samurai made it costly, and a Gashadokuro on the west kept the score respectable, but the Ashigaru never got back to the centre.
