The scoring record
Who scored the VP. At every scoring moment the engine was asked what would have scored with some of the side's units absent: a unit's share is its extra VP averaged over every order its side's units could have arrived in (its Shapley value), so the shares add up to the VP scored. "Hinged on it" is the objective VP its side would have lost had that one unit not been there, the rest of the battle as it was. VP no unit decided (an objective held by capture, a no-losses card): Judgement of Odin 6 VP.

Its fair share of the objective, Primary and secondary VP its side scored

Moves undone
A move the unit's next move undoes is a turn spent getting nowhere, as in chess when a piece goes and comes back while the other side develops. A pair counts when the second move takes the unit back beside where the first began, or away again from an objective the first moved toward, no nearer than it started. A CHARGE or a Fall Back is a fight's move and does not count. In this battle 2 of 67 moves were undone.
