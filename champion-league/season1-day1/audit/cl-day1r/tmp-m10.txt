Moves undone
A move the unit's next move undoes is a turn spent getting nowhere, as in chess when a piece goes and comes back while the other side develops. A pair counts when the second move takes the unit back beside where the first began, or away again from an objective the first moved toward, no nearer than it started. A CHARGE or a Fall Back is a fight's move and does not count. In this battle 7 of 60 moves were undone.
