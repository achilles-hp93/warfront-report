From the five batches of this day (`legion-vs-merlin`, `procession-vs-heron`, `taichou-vs-rurik`,
`ramesses-vs-valkyrie`, `genghis-vs-penitent`) and the work around them.

## Fixed

- **The page ran out of memory.** The unit list's row cache (game repo `3790d270`) keyed each row on its
  whole markup, which inlined the unit's icon and faction crest as data: URIs: keys of up to 900 KB,
  compared on every redraw. The page kept about 0.5 GB more each Battle Round in every matchup (after a
  forced GC: 0.6 GB in Round 1, 3.0 GB by Round 6 of Genghis vs Penitent, seed 1240), and V8 ran out in
  Round 7 of seeds 1240 and 1242 ("V8 javascript OOM" in Chrome's log; the recorder saw a CDP timeout).
  Fixed in `54e7aaa0`: the images are set on the row after parsing and compared by identity. The same
  battle holds 0.44 to 0.48 GB to Round 54.
- **The charged target's Fall Back check** (about a fifth of the AI's time): the phase-long answer (the
  approximation the user chose) reused 2 answers in two 4-round replays, so it saved nothing. An exact
  bound now asks the check only where a charge can still win: 36 to 13 and 28 to 9 checks on seeds 7 and
  11, the same fingerprints, about 12 percent of the AI's time.
